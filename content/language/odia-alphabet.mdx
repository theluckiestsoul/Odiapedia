# Odia Alphabet & Script

The **Odia script** (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଲିପି) is one of the most beautiful writing systems in India. Its distinctive rounded, curvilinear letters evolved over centuries from the Brahmi family of scripts.

## Why Are Odia Letters Round?

The most widely cited explanation is that Odia was traditionally written on **palm leaves** (tala patra) by incising them with a stylus. Long straight strokes tended to tear the leaves along their grain, so scribes developed smooth, flowing curves.

## The Odia Vowels (ସ୍ୱରବର୍ଣ୍ଣ)

The traditional Odia alphabet lists 14 vowels, although ୠ, ଌ and ୡ are rarely used in modern writing. Note that Odia pronunciation differs from Hindi: ଅ is a rounded sound, and ଇ/ଈ and ଉ/ଊ are not usually distinguished by length in everyday speech.

| Letter | Name | Transliteration | Sound |
|--------|------|-----------------|-------|
| ଅ | ଅ | a | rounded, like "aw" in "saw" (shorter) |
| ଆ | ଆ | ā | as in "father" |
| ଇ | ଇ | i | as in "sit" or "see" |
| ଈ | ଈ | ī | as in "see" |
| ଉ | ଉ | u | as in "put" or "moon" |
| ଊ | ଊ | ū | as in "moon" |
| ଋ | ଋ | ṛ | pronounced "ru" |
| ୠ | ୠ | ṝ | rare |
| ଌ | ଌ | ḷ | rare |
| ୡ | ୡ | ḹ | rare |
| ଏ | ଏ | e | as in "eight" |
| ଐ | ଐ | ai | pronounced "oi" |
| ଓ | ଓ | o | as in "go" |
| ଔ | ଔ | au | pronounced "ou" |

## The Odia Consonants (ବ୍ୟଞ୍ଜନବର୍ଣ୍ଣ)

Odia consonants are organized into groups (ବର୍ଗ) by place of articulation, followed by unstructured consonants. Counts of 35 to 38 consonants are common, depending on the convention used:

### Velar Consonants (କବର୍ଗ)
**କ ଖ ଗ ଘ ଙ**

### Palatal Consonants (ଚବର୍ଗ)
**ଚ ଛ ଜ ଝ ଞ**

### Retroflex Consonants (ଟବର୍ଗ)
**ଟ ଠ ଡ ଢ ଣ**

### Dental Consonants (ତବର୍ଗ)
**ତ ଥ ଦ ଧ ନ**

### Labial Consonants (ପବର୍ଗ)
**ପ ଫ ବ ଭ ମ**

### Semi-vowels & Others
**ଯ ୟ ର ଲ ଳ ୱ ଶ ଷ ସ ହ**

Odia also uses the retroflex flaps **ଡ଼** and **ଢ଼**, and many alphabet charts add the conjunct **କ୍ଷ** at the end.

## Special Characters

- **ଂ** (Anuswara) — Nasal sound
- **ଃ** (Visarga) — Aspirate
- **ଁ** (Chandrabindu) — Nasalization

## Common Conjuncts (ଯୁକ୍ତାକ୍ଷର)

When consonants combine, they form conjuncts:
- **କ୍ଷ** (kṣa)
- **ତ୍ର** (tra)
- **ଜ୍ଞ** (jña)
- **ଶ୍ର** (śra)

## Tips for Learning

1. **Start with vowels** — Master the commonly used vowels before moving to consonants
2. **Practice curves** — Use calligraphy pens for authentic strokes
3. **Read simple words** — Children's books are great for beginners
4. **Watch the hand** — YouTube videos of native writers help with stroke order
