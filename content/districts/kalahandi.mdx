## At a Glance
Kalahandi ("Pot of Arts") is famous for its rich history, tribal culture, and gemstones (the area was historically called Karunda Mandal for its precious stones). Though it has faced challenges of drought in the past, it is a land of breathtaking natural beauty with waterfalls, mountains, and lush valleys like **Thuamul Rampur**.

## The Heritage Diary
The region is identified with Mahakantara ("great forest"), a forest territory named in the Allahabad pillar inscription of Samudragupta. Archaeological sites like **Asurgarh** reveal an urban settlement of about 2,000 years ago.

## Local Landmarks
- **Manikeswari Temple:** The temple of the presiding deity of the royal family in Bhawanipatna. The **Chhatar Jatra** festival here is well known.
- **Phurlijharan:** A perennial waterfall located close to Bhawanipatna, a favorite picnic spot.
- **Karlapat Wildlife Sanctuary:** A dense forest with diverse wildlife and waterfalls.
- **Dokarichanchara:** A scenic spot with waterfalls and historical significance.
- **Ampani:** A hilly area known for its scenic views.

## Taste of the Land
- **Millet-based dishes:** Ragi (Mandia) is a staple crop and used in porridge and cakes.

## Local Heroes
- **Bhubaneswar Behera:** Engineer, educationist and Odia writer, born at Kashibahal in the former Kalahandi state.
