## At a Glance
Gajapati district was carved out of Ganjam and is named after Maharaja Krushna Chandra Gajapati Narayan Deo, who became the first Premier (Prime Minister) of Orissa province in 1937. The district is known for its rich tribal culture (Soura tribe), agro-products, and the historic town of Paralakhemundi.

## The Heritage Diary
Paralakhemundi was a significant center of art and culture. Maharaja Krushna Chandra Gajapati played a leading part in the formation of the separate province of Odisha in 1936. The Saura (Sora) people of the district are known for their traditional wall paintings.

## Local Landmarks
- **Mahendragiri:** The highest peak of the district (about 1,501 m), mentioned in the Ramayana and Mahabharata. Its ancient temples are associated by tradition with the Pandavas, and it is popular with trekkers.
- **Paralakhemundi Palace:** The palace of the former Paralakhemundi rulers in the district headquarters town.
- **Gandahati Waterfall:** A waterfall surrounded by dense forests.
- **Jirang:** Known as "Little Tibet," it houses a large Tibetan settlement and the majestic Padmasambhava Monastery.

## Taste of the Land
- **Tribal Cuisine:** Millet-based dishes and organic vegetables are staples in the rural areas.

## Local Heroes
- **Maharaja Krushna Chandra Gajapati:** A key figure in the creation of the separate Odisha province in 1936.
