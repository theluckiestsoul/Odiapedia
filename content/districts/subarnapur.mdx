## At a Glance
The name Subarnapur (Sonepur) means "city of gold." It is situated at the confluence of the Mahanadi and Tel rivers. It is known for its handloom sarees (**Bomkai** and Sonepuri patta) and its many temples, which have earned it the popular name "Second Varanasi."

## The Heritage Diary
Sonepur was one of the two capitals of the Somavamsi dynasty around the 10th–11th centuries. It was later a princely state. The region is rich in antiquities and has a strong literary tradition.

## Local Landmarks
- **Lankeswari:** A temple of the region's historic presiding goddess, on a rock in the Mahanadi river bed.
- **Patali Sreekhetra:** A site at Kotsamlai where, according to tradition, Lord Jagannath was hidden to protect him from invaders.

## Taste of the Land
- **Fish:** Being at a confluence, fresh river fish is a staple.

## Local Heroes
- **Bhima Bhoi:** The saint-poet of Mahima Dharma, who established his ashram at Khaliapali near Sonepur.
- **Chaturbhuj Meher:** Master weaver honoured with the Padma Shri.
