## At a Glance
Kandhamal is a district of rolling hills, pine jungles, and waterfalls. It is known for its organic turmeric, Kandhamal Haladi, which received a Geographical Indication (GI) tag in 2019. **Daringbadi**, located here, is known as the "Kashmir of Odisha" due to its cool climate and winter frost.

## The Heritage Diary
The district takes its name from the Kandha (Kondh) people, who make up roughly half of its population.

## Local Landmarks
- **Daringbadi:** A hill station surrounded by pine forests, coffee gardens, and waterfalls. It is a major tourist destination.
- **Phulbani:** The district headquarters, known for its scenic beauty and Pilasalunki dam.
- **Balaskumpa:** Famous for the temple of Goddess Barala Devi.
- **Belghar:** A forested upland with a nature camp, in an area inhabited by the Kutia Kandha community.

## Taste of the Land
- **Kandhamal Haldi:** Not a dish, but the spice that flavors everything.

## Local Heroes
- The tribal leaders who have preserved the indigenous culture and traditions of the region.
