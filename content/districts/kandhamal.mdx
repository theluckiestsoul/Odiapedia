## At a Glance
Kandhamal is a district of rolling hills, pine jungles, and waterfalls. It is naturally gifted and is famous for its organic turmeric, which recently received a GI tag. **Daringbadi**, located here, is known as the "Kashmir of Odisha" due to its cool climate and winter frost.

## The Heritage Diary
The district is the homeland of the Kandha tribe. It played a role in the Bhanja dynasty's rule. The region has a rich tradition of Dokra casting and terra-cotta works.

## Local Landmarks
- **Daringbadi:** A hill station surrounded by pine forests, coffee gardens, and waterfalls. It is a major tourist destination.
- **Phulbani:** The district headquarters, known for its scenic beauty and Pilasalunki dam.
- **Balaskumpa:** Famous for the temple of Goddess Barala Devi.
- **Belghar:** A nature camp and sanctuary for the Kutia Kandha tribe.

## Taste of the Land
- **Kandhamal Haldi:** Not a dish, but the spice that flavors everything.
- **Jhudo Pithaa:** A local tribal delicacy made with millets and jaggery.

## Local Heroes
- The tribal leaders who have preserved the indigenous culture and traditions of the region.
