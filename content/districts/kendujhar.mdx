## At a Glance
Kendujhar (Keonjhar) is a district of hills, waterfalls, and minerals. It sits on the mineral-rich Chota Nagpur plateau. Scheduled Tribes make up about 45% of its population (Census 2011), including the Juang and Bhuyan communities.

## The Heritage Diary
Keonjhar was a princely state ruled by the Bhanja dynasty. It was the site of the **Bhuyan Meli** (rebellion) of 1891, led by Dharanidhar Naik against forced labour and feudal exactions.

## Local Landmarks
- **Ghatagaon Tarini Temple:** One of the most revered Shakti shrines in Odisha. The coconuts offered here are transported from all over the state.
- **Sanaghagara & Badaghagara:** Two stunning waterfalls located close to the district headquarters.
- **Khandadhar:** A high waterfall, distinct from the Khandadhar falls of Sundargarh district.
- **Gonasika:** The source of the Baitarani river, emerging from a cow-head shaped rock.
- **Sitabinji:** Known for the fresco painting in the Ravana Chhaya rock shelter, dated to about the 5th century CE.

## Taste of the Land
- **Badi:** Sun-dried lentil dumplings, including flower-shaped Phula Badi, are a pantry staple.
- **Mushroom Curry:** Wild mushrooms collected from the forests are a delicacy during monsoons.

## Local Heroes
- **Dharanidhar Naik:** A tribal leader who led the uprising against feudal oppression. A university in Keonjhar is named after him.
