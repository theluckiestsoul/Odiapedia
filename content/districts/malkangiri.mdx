## At a Glance
Malkangiri is the southernmost district of Odisha, bordering Andhra Pradesh and Chhattisgarh. It is a land of dense forests, hills, and deep reservoirs. It is home to the **Bonda** people, classified as a Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Group (PVTG).

## The Heritage Diary
Legend associates Malkangiri with the Ramayana; Sitakunda and other spots are believed to be where Sita bathed during her exile. In the Ramayana tradition, the region was known as Malyavantagiri.

## Local Landmarks
- **Balimela Reservoir:** A massive hydroelectric project on the Sileru river. The cut-off area (Swabhiman Anchal) is now connected by the Gurupriya bridge.
- **Satiguda Dam:** A dam and reservoir near the district headquarters.
- **Bonda Hills:** The isolated home of the Bonda tribe, known for their unique culture and attire.
- **Motu:** The southernmost point of Odisha where the Sabari and Sileru rivers meet.

## Taste of the Land
- **Bamboo Shoot Curry:** A favorite among the tribal communities.
- **Millet Gruel:** A staple diet in the hilly regions.

## Local Heroes
- **Tama Dora:** A revolutionary tribal leader who fought against the British in the Koya rebellion.
