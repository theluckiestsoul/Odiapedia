## At a Glance
Puri is the holiest district in Odisha and one of the four most sacred pilgrimage sites (Char Dham) for Hindus. It is the abode of **Lord Jagannath**, the Lord of the Universe. It is bounded by the Bay of Bengal, offering pristine beaches.

## The Heritage Diary
Puri's history is synonymous with the history of the Jagannath Temple, built in the 12th century by Anantavarman Chodaganga Deva. The nearby **Konark Sun Temple**, a UNESCO World Heritage site built by Narasimhadeva I, is a masterpiece of Kalinga architecture representing the sun god's chariot.

## Local Landmarks
- **Jagannath Temple:** The heart of Odia culture. The annual Ratha Yatra attracts millions.
- **Konark Sun Temple:** The Black Pagoda, a marvel of stone carving and astronomy.
- **Golden Beach:** A Blue Flag certified beach, clean and tourist-friendly.
- **Raghurajpur:** A heritage crafts village famous for Pattachitra paintings and Gotipua dance.
- **Pipili:** Famous for its colorful applique work.

## Taste of the Land
- **Mahaprasad:** The holy food offered to Lord Jagannath, cooked in earthen pots. The temple kitchen is one of the largest in the world.
- **Khaja:** A crispy, layered sweet delight.
- **Malpua:** A sweet pancake.

## Local Heroes
- **Gopabandhu Das:** The "Utkalmani" (Jewel of Utkal), a selfless social worker, freedom fighter, and writer born in Suando.
- **Kelucharan Mohapatra:** The legendary Odissi dance guru born in Raghurajpur.
