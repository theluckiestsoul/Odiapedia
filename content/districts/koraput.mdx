## At a Glance
Koraput is a paradise of nature located in the Eastern Ghats. It is known for its misty hills, waterfalls, and terraced valleys. Its Jagannath temple, known as "Sabara Srikhetra," is an important regional shrine.

## The Heritage Diary
The district is home to large Paraja, Kandha, Bhottada and Gadaba communities. It was part of the Jeypore kingdom. The region has a rich history of resistance against British rule, with tribal uprisings headed by Laxman Nayak.

## Local Landmarks
- **Deomali:** The highest mountain peak in Odisha (1672 m), offering breathtaking views.
- **Gupteswar:** A famous cave shrine of Lord Shiva located in deep forests.
- **Duduma Waterfall:** A waterfall on the Machkund (Sileru) river, falling about 540 feet.
- **Sabara Srikhetra:** The Jagannath temple in Koraput town, built to promote social integration.
- **Jeypore:** The historic city of the region with an old palace.

## Taste of the Land
- **Koraput Coffee:** Coffee is grown on the Koraput hills.
- **Kalajeera Rice:** Koraput Kalajeera, an aromatic rice, received a GI tag in January 2024.
- **Mandia Peja:** A millet soup that is a staple diet of the tribal people.

## Local Heroes
- **Saheed Laxman Nayak:** A tribal freedom fighter from Tentuligumma, hanged in Berhampur Jail on 29 March 1943.
