## At a Glance
Koraput is a paradise of nature located in the Eastern Ghats. It is known for its misty hills, waterfalls, and terraced valleys. It is often called the "Sabara Srikhetra" due to its Jagannath temple which allows entry to all castes and tribes.

## The Heritage Diary
The district is the cradle of the Paraja and Gadaba tribes. It was part of the Jeypore kingdom. The region has a rich history of resistance against British rule, with tribal uprisings headed by Laxman Nayak.

## Local Landmarks
- **Deomali:** The highest mountain peak in Odisha (1672 m), offering breathtaking views.
- **Gupteswar:** A famous cave shrine of Lord Shiva located in deep forests.
- **Duduma Waterfall:** A majestic waterfall on the Machkund river.
- **Sabara Srikhetra:** The Jagannath temple in Koraput town, built to promote social integration.
- **Jeypore:** The historic city of the region with an old palace.

## Taste of the Land
- **Koraput Coffee:** Organic coffee grown in the hills is gaining global recognition.
- **Mandia Peja:** A millet soup that is a staple diet of the tribal people.

## Local Heroes
- **Saheed Laxman Nayak:** A legendary tribal freedom fighter who was hanged by the British.
