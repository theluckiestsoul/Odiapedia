## At a Glance
Kendrapara is bounded by the Bay of Bengal to the east. It is a fertile land crisscrossed by rivers. It is spiritually significant as **Tulasi Khetra** and ecologically significant for the Bhitarkanika mangroves.

## The Heritage Diary
According to local tradition, Tulasi was the consort of Lord Balabhadra, which gives Kendrapara the name Tulasi Khetra. The district has a long history of maritime activity. It is also known for its political consciousness.

## Local Landmarks
- **Baladevjew Temple:** The famous temple dedicated to Lord Balabhadra (the elder brother of Jagannath). Its annual Ratha Yatra is a major event.
- **Bhitarkanika National Park:** A Ramsar site and one of India's largest mangrove ecosystems. It is famous for its giant Saltwater Crocodiles and migratory birds.
- **Gahirmatha:** One of the world's most important mass-nesting sites for Olive Ridley sea turtles.
- **Pentha Beach:** Known for its geo-tube sea wall and pristine sands.

## Taste of the Land
- **Rasabali:** A sweet dish that originated at the Baladevjew temple. It consists of fried cheese disks soaked in thickened, sweetened milk.
- **Potala Rasa:** Pointed gourd curry, a local favorite.

## Local Heroes
- **Biju Patnaik:** The former Chief Minister represented the Kendrapara Lok Sabha constituency from 1977 to 1985.
