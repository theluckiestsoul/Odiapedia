## At a Glance
Kendrapara is bounded by the Bay of Bengal to the east. It is a fertile land crisscrossed by rivers. It is spiritually significant as **Tulasi Khetra** and ecologically significant for the Bhitarkanika mangroves.

## The Heritage Diary
Legend has it that Lord Balabhadra married Tulasi (the daughter of a demon king) here. The district has a long history of maritime activity. It is also known for its political consciousness.

## Local Landmarks
- **Baladevjew Temple:** The famous temple dedicated to Lord Balabhadra (the elder brother of Jagannath). The Ratha Yatra here is massive.
- **Bhitarkanika National Park:** A Ramsar site and India's second-largest mangrove ecosystem. It is famous for its giant Saltwater Crocodiles and migratory birds.
- **Gahirmatha:** The world's largest nesting beach for Olive Ridley sea turtles.
- **Pentha Beach:** Known for its geo-tube sea wall and pristine sands.

## Taste of the Land
- **Rasabali:** A heavenly sweet dish originating from the Baladevjew temple. It consists of fried cheese disks soaked in thickened, sweetened milk.
- **Potala Rasa:** Pointed gourd curry, a local favorite.

## Local Heroes
- **Biju Patnaik:** The legendary leader represented this constituency for many years, making it his fortress.
