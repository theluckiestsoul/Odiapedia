## At a Glance
Jagatsinghpur is the smallest district in Odisha by area but packs a punch with its cultural and economic significance. It is home to **Paradeep**, one of India's major ports, facilitating trade and industry. The district marks the end of the Mahanadi river's journey as it meets the Bay of Bengal.

## The Heritage Diary
The district has a rich maritime history, being part of the ancient Kalinga trade routes to Southeast Asia. The statewide **Boita Bandana** festival recalls this maritime past. It is also the land of **Sarala Das**, revered as the Adikabi (first poet) of Odia literature and author of the Odia Mahabharata, who is said to have been born at Kanakapura in this region.

## Local Landmarks
- **Sarala Temple:** The shrine of Goddess Sarala (Saraswati/Durga) in Jhankad. It is a major center of literature and learning.
- **Paradeep Port:** A bustling major port with a beautiful beach and marine aquarium.
- **Gorakhnath Temple:** A shrine in Jagatsinghpur town dedicated to Guru Gorakhnath of the Nath tradition.
- **Gahirmatha:** Nearby (politically in Kendrapara but accessible), famous for Olive Ridley turtle nesting.

## Taste of the Land
- **Seafood:** Fresh crabs and prawns from Paradeep are popular.

## Local Heroes
- **Adikabi Sarala Das:** The 15th-century poet who laid the foundation of Odia literature.
- **Nabakrushna Choudhury:** Freedom fighter and former Chief Minister, born at Kherasa village, now in Jagatsinghpur district.
