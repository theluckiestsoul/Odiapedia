## At a Glance
Sambalpur is the gateway to western Odisha. It is widely known for its **Sambalpuri Sarees** (Bandha Kala). The name is traditionally linked to the presiding goddess Samaleswari. It houses the **Hirakud Dam**, often described as the longest earthen dam in the world.

## The Heritage Diary
Sambalpur has a distinct cultural identity. It was a strong center of resistance against the British under Veer Surendra Sai. It was historically known for its diamond trade (Hirakhanda).

## Local Landmarks
- **Samaleswari Temple:** The abode of Maa Samaleswari, the presiding deity of western Odisha.
- **Hirakud Dam:** A colossal engineering marvel on the Mahanadi river. The dyke offers stunning views. Gandhi Minar and Nehru Minar are viewpoints.
- **Huma (Leaning Temple):** A unique temple dedicated to Lord Bimaleswar which leans at an angle.
- **Ghanteswari:** A temple where thousands of bells are offered by devotees.

## Taste of the Land
- **Chaula Bara:** The crispy rice dumplings are a staple snack.

## Local Heroes
- **Veer Surendra Sai:** The freedom fighter, born at Khinda near Sambalpur, who spent about 37 years in British prisons.
- **Bhima Bhoi:** The saint-poet of Mahima Dharma; one account places his birth at Madhupur in Rairakhol, though sources differ.
