## At a Glance
Sundargarh is a district of industries and sports. It houses **Rourkela**, the "Steel City of Odisha," home to one of India's early public-sector steel plants. It is also widely regarded as a nursery of Indian hockey, producing legends like Dilip Tirkey.

## The Heritage Diary
The district was formed on 1 January 1948 from the former princely states of Gangpur and Bonai. The district is rich in minerals like iron ore, manganese, and limestone. It has a significant tribal population (Oraon, Munda, Kisan, Kharia and others).

## Local Landmarks
- **Rourkela Steel Plant:** A massive industrial complex that modernized the region.
- **Hanuman Vatika:** A garden in Rourkela with a very tall statue of Lord Hanuman.
- **Khandadhar Falls:** The other Khandadhar (different from Keonjhar's), located in Nandapani. It is a spectacular waterfall.
- **Vedvyas:** Located at the confluence of Sankh, Koel, and Saraswati rivers. According to tradition, Sage Vyasa composed the Mahabharata here.

## Taste of the Land
- **Handia:** A rice beer popular among tribals.
- **Modern Cuisine:** Rourkela's cosmopolitan nature offers food from all over India.

## Local Heroes
- **Dilip Tirkey:** Former captain of the Indian Hockey team and a legend of the game.
- **Ignace Tirkey:** Another hockey great.
- **Lazarus Barla:** Hockey player who represented India.
