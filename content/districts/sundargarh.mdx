## At a Glance
Sundargarh is a district of industries and sports. It houses **Rourkela**, the "Steel City of Odisha," with one of the first steel plants in India. It is also the cradle of Indian Hockey, producing legends like Dilip Tirkey.

## The Heritage Diary
Originally a princely state (Gangpur), it merged with Odisha in 1948. The district is rich in minerals like iron ore, manganese, and limestone. It has a significant tribal population (Oram, Kisan, Munda).

## Local Landmarks
- **Rourkela Steel Plant:** A massive industrial complex that modernized the region.
- **Hanuman Vatika:** A garden in Rourkela with a colossal 75-foot statue of Lord Hanuman.
- **Khandadhar Falls:** The other Khandadhar (different from Keonjhar's), located in Nandapani. It is a spectacular waterfall.
- **Vedvyas:** Located at the confluence of Sankh, Koel, and Saraswati rivers. It is believed to be the place where Sage Vyasa wrote the Mahabharata.

## Taste of the Land
- **Handia:** A rice beer popular among tribals.
- **Modern Cuisine:** Rourkela's cosmopolitan nature offers food from all over India.

## Local Heroes
- **Dilip Tirkey:** Former captain of the Indian Hockey team and a legend of the game.
- **Ignace Tirkey:** Another hockey great.
- **Lazarus Barla:** Hockey player. Basically, the whole Indian defense line often comes from here!
