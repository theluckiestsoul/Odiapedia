## At a Glance
Boudh is a centrally located district in Odisha, historically known as a center of Buddhism and Shaivism. The Mahanadi river forms its northern and western boundaries. It is known for its peaceful ambiance, handloom weaving, and agricultural lifestyle.

## The Heritage Diary
The name "Boudh" is generally linked to Buddhism. The region was an important Buddhist centre for centuries, as evidenced by the large Buddha image still present in the town. Under the Bhanja rulers and later the Somavamsis, Shaivism flourished, leading to the construction of unique star-shaped temples.

## Local Landmarks
- **Ramanath Temples:** A group of three ancient temples dedicated to Lord Shiva, known for their star-shaped plans and intricate carvings, near the Mahanadi.
- **Boudh Buddha Statue:** A colossal image of Buddha in the Bhumisparsha Mudra, a testament to the region's Buddhist past.
- **Padmatola Sanctuary:** A wildlife sanctuary in the district, home to diverse flora and fauna.
- **Puruna Katak:** Another historical site known for its temples and the shrine of Goddess Bhairavi.

## Taste of the Land
Boudh is known for its simple, rustic Odia cuisine.
- **Machha Bhaja:** Fried river fish from the Mahanadi is a staple.
- **Pithas:** Various types of rice cakes prepared during festivals.

## Local Heroes
- The district has produced many freedom fighters and social reformers who contributed to the development of the region, although they may not be as widely known nationally.
