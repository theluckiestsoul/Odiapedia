## At a Glance
Jajpur is a district that perfectly blends ancient history with modern industrialization. While Kalinga Nagar has grown into a major steel-making centre, the town of Jajpur remains a timeless pilgrimage site known as **Biraja Khetra** or **Gada Khetra**. The district is rich in archaeological finds.

## The Heritage Diary
Jajpur was an important seat of power in early medieval Odisha; the district administration credits its foundation to the Somavamsi king Yayati (Jajati) Keshari in the early 10th century. It is identified with the Viraja Tirtha of the Mahabharata. The region is dotted with ancient Buddhist sites like Ratnagiri and Udayagiri, part of the famous "Diamond Triangle" of Odisha.

## Local Landmarks
- **Biraja Temple:** Revered as a Shakti Peetha, dedicated to Goddess Biraja. The idol is unique as a two-armed Durga slaying Mahishasura.
- **Ratnagiri & Udayagiri:** Ancient Buddhist monastic complexes with stupas and exquisite sculptures.
- **Dasaswamedha Ghat:** A historic ghat on the Baitarani river, considered very holy.

## Taste of the Land
- **Chhena Jhili:** A sweet associated with Nimapada in Puri district, also found in local sweet shops.
- **Local River Fish:** The Baitarani river provides fresh fish which is a staple.

## Local Heroes
- Generally, the rulers of the Bhaumakar and Somavamsi dynasties are the historical heroes who built the magnificent structures.
