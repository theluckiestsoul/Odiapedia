## At a Glance
Dhenkanal is a district known for its gently undulating hills and spiritual significance. It is known for its Dhokra metal casting. The district is a blend of history, religion, and natural beauty.

## The Heritage Diary
Dhenkanal was a princely state ruled by the Singh Deo dynasty. It played a pivotal role in the freedom movement, notably the killing of Baji Rout, often remembered as one of the youngest martyrs of India's freedom struggle. The **Mahima Dharma**, a monotheistic sect, has its main seat (gadi) at Joranda in this district.

## Local Landmarks
- **Kapilas:** Regarded by some as a second "Kailash" of Shiva, this hill houses the temple of Lord Chandrasekhar (Shiva) at a height of 2239 feet. A winding road and a flight of 1352 steps lead to the top.
- **Joranda Gadhi:** The spiritual headquarters of the Mahima Dharma. The Magha Mela held here draws monks and devotees from across the country.
- **Saptasajya:** A scenic spot named after the seven hills surrounding it. According to local tradition, the Pandavas spent some time here during their exile.

## Taste of the Land
- **Magji:** A sweet made from buffalo-milk chhena, associated with Dhenkanal.

## Local Heroes
- **Baji Rout:** The 12-year-old boatman who was shot dead by police on 11 October 1938 at Nilakanthapur Ghat near Bhuban for refusing to ferry them across the Brahmani river.
- **Sarangadhar Das:** A prominent freedom fighter and socialist leader, born in the Dhenkanal princely state.
