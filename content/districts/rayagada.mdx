## At a Glance
Rayagada is a land of meadows, forests, waterfalls, and terraced valleys. It is predominantly inhabited by tribals, most notably the **Dongria Kondhs** who live in the Niyamgiri hills. It is also an industrial hub with ferro-alloy plants and paper mills.

## The Heritage Diary
Rayagada was part of the Kalinga region and later the Jeypore (Nandapur) kingdom; Vishwanath Dev Gajapati of Nandapur (1527–1571 CE) is credited with founding a capital on the Nagavali and naming it Rayagada. The region has a rich tradition of tribal art and jewelry.

## Local Landmarks
- **Maa Majhighariani Temple:** The presiding deity of the region, highly revered.
- **Chatikona:** A village about 48 km from Rayagada with a waterfall and a Shiva temple, near the Niyamgiri hills.
- **Hanging Bridge:** A suspension bridge over the Nagavali river at Chekaguda, described as Odisha's second simple suspension bridge.
- **Minajhola:** Situated at the confluence of the Vamsadhara, Chauladhua and Phalaphalia rivers, it is a Shiva pilgrimage site.

## Taste of the Land
- **Salap:** Palm sap, drunk fresh or fermented, popular among tribal communities.

## Local Heroes
Leaders from the tribal community who have represented the region's interests.
