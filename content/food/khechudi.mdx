recipe:
  yield: 4 servings
  prep: 15
  cook: 35
  course: Main course
  diet:
  - Vegetarian
  ingredients:
  - 1 cup short-grain aromatic rice
  - ½ cup split yellow moong dal, dry-roasted
  - 5 cups water
  - 3 tbsp ghee
  - 1 bay leaf, 1 tsp cumin seeds, ¼ tsp asafoetida
  - 1 tsp grated ginger
  - ½ tsp turmeric, 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper, rock salt
  - ⅓ cup grated fresh coconut
  - 1 tsp jaggery or sugar
  steps:
  - Wash and soak the rice and dal together 15–20 minutes; drain.
  - Boil the water, add rice, dal, turmeric, ginger and salt and simmer, partly covered, about 20 minutes.
  - Add the coconut and pepper and cook on low 8–10 minutes until soft and porridge-like.
  - Heat 2 tbsp ghee with the bay leaf, cumin and asafoetida and pour over.
  - Stir in the jaggery and remaining ghee, rest 5 minutes and serve.
  tips:
  - Mitha khechudi, the sweet version, adds more ghee, sugar, cardamom and dry fruit.

**Khechudi** (ଖେଚୁଡ଼ି) is the Odia khichdi: rice and lentils cooked soft with ghee. It is one of the cooked dishes of the [Jagannath Temple](/history/jagannath-temple)'s **Mahaprasad**, prepared in earthen pots over firewood in the Rosaghara under strict *satvik* rules — no onion, no garlic. Home "temple-style" khechudi is seasoned with ginger, asafoetida, black pepper and grated coconut and finished with cow's ghee; **mitha khechudi** is a sweeter festive version with more ghee, sugar and dry fruit.
