recipe:
  yield: 6 servings
  prep: 30
  cook: 45
  rest: 480
  rest_label: Soaking the legumes
  course: Main course
  diet:
  - Vegetarian
  ingredients:
  - ⅓ cup chana dal or dried chickpeas, soaked overnight; ¼ cup sprouted moong (optional)
  - '4–5 cups mixed vegetables: raw banana, pumpkin, sweet potato, taro, yam, brinjal, ridge gourd, papaya, beans, spine gourd, elephant apple (ou) if available'
  - ½ tsp turmeric and salt
  - 3 tbsp mustard oil
  - 1 tsp panch phutana, 2 dried red chillies, 1 bay leaf
  - 1-inch ginger, ground
  - 'Mustard paste: 1 tbsp mustard seeds and 2 green chillies ground with a little water'
  - 'Bhaja masala: 1 tsp cumin and 2 red chillies, roasted and ground'
  - ¼ cup grated coconut and 1 tsp ghee
  steps:
  - Cook the soaked chana and moong until soft but whole.
  - Par-boil the hard vegetables (yam, taro, sweet potato, raw banana) with turmeric and salt for 5–6 minutes; taro and yam must be cooked through.
  - Heat the oil and fry the panch phutana, chillies and bay leaf, then the ginger.
  - Add all the vegetables and fry 5 minutes; add the mustard paste, legumes and about 1½ cups water. Cover and simmer 15–20 minutes until thick and partly mashed.
  - Stir in the bhaja masala and coconut and finish with ghee.
  tips:
  - The more kinds of vegetable, the better — use what is in season.
  - Elephant apple gives the traditional sourness; a little tamarind can stand in.

**Ghanta** (ghanta tarkari) is a thick, hearty curry made from as many vegetables as the season offers, cooked with legumes in a mustard-paste gravy. Kalinga TV reports that it is cooked in homes across Odisha **the day after Dutibahana Osha** — called **Pua Jiuntia** in western Odisha — and lists raw plantain, ridge gourd, pumpkin, taro, elephant apple (*ou*), sweet potato, coconut, sprouted green gram, beans, spine gourd, chickpea, carrot, brinjal, papaya and yam among its vegetables.
