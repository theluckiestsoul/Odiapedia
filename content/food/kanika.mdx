recipe:
  yield: 3–4 servings

  ingredients:
  - 1 cup short-grain aromatic rice (Gobindobhoga or similar)
  - 3–4 tbsp ghee
  - ¼ tsp turmeric and ½ tsp salt
  - 1 bay leaf, 1-inch cinnamon, 3 green cardamoms, 1 black cardamom, 4 cloves, a blade of mace, 6 peppercorns
  - 2 cups hot water
  - ¼ cup sugar, or to taste
  - 12 cashews and 1 tbsp raisins
  - A pinch of nutmeg and ½ tsp grated ginger (optional)
  steps:
  - Soak the rice 30 minutes and drain. Rub in 1 tbsp ghee, the turmeric and salt and rest 20–30 minutes.
  - Fry the cashews and raisins golden in the remaining ghee and lift out. Fry the whole spices 30 seconds in the same ghee.
  - Add the rice and fry gently 2–3 minutes, then the hot water. Cover and cook on low until three-quarters done.
  - Scatter the sugar over, cover and cook on the lowest heat 5–8 minutes until the water is absorbed.
  - Fold in the nuts and nutmeg and rest covered 5 minutes. Serve with dalma.
  tips:
  - For bhoga style use only ghee and no onion or garlic.
  - Adjust the sugar to taste; festive versions are sweeter.

**Kanika** is a fragrant sweet rice cooked with ghee, sugar and whole spices and studded with cashews and raisins. It is part of the **Mahaprasad** of the [Jagannath Temple](/history/jagannath-temple) in Puri, where it is offered at **Sakala Dhupa**, the morning offering. Temple food is cooked in earthen pots over firewood in the Rosaghara (temple kitchen) without onion or garlic. At home kanika is a festive rice, eaten with [dalma](/food/dalma) or besara.
