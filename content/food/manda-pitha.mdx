**Manda pitha** (ମଣ୍ଡା ପିଠା) is a soft, steamed dumpling of rice flour with a sweet filling — most often coconut and jaggery with cardamom and a little black pepper. The name is linked to the Odia *mandeiba*, "to place or put". It is a festival pitha, made for **Manabasa Gurubara, Durga Puja, Kumar Purnima, Rakhi Purnima** and **Raja**, and is a cousin of Maharashtra's modak and South India's kozhukattai. Western Odisha also has a fried version with a semolina shell.

## The filling

Coconut and jaggery are cooked together until thick; cardamom and black pepper are the traditional seasonings. Some families add moong dal or crumbled **chhena**, and savoury mandas use a spiced lentil or vegetable filling.
