Now let's learn phrases you'll use in real life - at shops, restaurants, and when asking for directions.

## At a Restaurant / Eating

| English | Odia | Pronunciation |
|---------|------|---------------|
| I am hungry | ମୋତେ ଭୋକ ଲାଗିଛି | Mote bhoka lāgichi |
| I am thirsty | ମୋତେ ତୃଷା ଲାଗିଛି | Mote tṛṣā lāgichi |
| Water, please | ପାଣି ଦିଅନ୍ତୁ | Pāṇi diʼantu |
| The food is delicious | ଖାଇବା ବହୁତ ସୁଆଦିଆ | Khāibā bahuta suādiā |
| I want rice | ମୋତେ ଭାତ ଚାହିଁ | Mote bhāta cāhiṁ |
| Bill, please | ବିଲ୍ ଦିଅନ୍ତୁ | Bil diʼantu |

## Shopping Phrases

| English | Odia | Pronunciation |
|---------|------|---------------|
| How much is this? | ଏହା କେତେ? | Ehā kete? |
| Too expensive! | ବହୁତ ମହଙ୍ଗା! | Bahuta mahaṅgā! |
| Less please | କମ୍ କରନ୍ତୁ | Kam karantu |
| I will buy this | ମୁଁ ଏହା କିଣିବି | Muṁ ehā kiṇibi |
| I don't want this | ମୋତେ ଏହା ଦରକାର ନାହିଁ | Mote ehā darakāra nāhiṁ |
| Do you have...? | ...ଅଛି କି? | ...achi ki? |

## Asking for Directions

| English | Odia | Pronunciation |
|---------|------|---------------|
| Where is...? | ...କେଉଁଠି? | ...keuṁṭhi? |
| Where is the station? | ଷ୍ଟେସନ କେଉଁଠି? | Sṭeśan keuṁṭhi? |
| Go straight | ସିଧା ଯାଆନ୍ତୁ | Sidhā yāʼāntu |
| Turn left | ବାମକୁ ବୁଲନ୍ତୁ | Bāmaku bulantu |
| Turn right | ଡାହାଣକୁ ବୁଲନ୍ତୁ | Ḍāhāṇaku bulantu |
| How far? | କେତେ ଦୂର? | Kete dūra? |

## Practice Scenario: At a Shop

**You**: ନମସ୍କାର! ଏହା କେତେ?
*(Hello! How much is this?)*

**Seller**: ପଚାଶ ରୁପିଆ।
*(Fifty rupees.)*

**You**: ବହୁତ ମହଙ୍ଗା! କମ୍ କରନ୍ତୁ।
*(Too expensive! Less please.)*

**Seller**: ଚାଳିଶ ରୁପିଆ।
*(Forty rupees.)*

**You**: ଠିକ୍ ଅଛି। ମୁଁ ଏହା କିଣିବି। ଧନ୍ୟବାଦ!
*(Okay. I will buy this. Thank you!)*
