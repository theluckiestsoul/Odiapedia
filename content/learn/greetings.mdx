Greetings are the foundation of any conversation. Let's learn how to say hello, goodbye, and be polite in Odia!

## Basic Greetings

| English | Odia | Pronunciation |
|---------|------|---------------|
| Hello / Namaste | ନମସ୍କାର | Namaskāra |
| Good morning | ଶୁଭ ସକାଳ | Śubha sakāḷa |
| Good evening | ଶୁଭ ସନ୍ଧ୍ୟା | Śubha sandhyā |
| Good night | ଶୁଭ ରାତ୍ରି | Śubha rātri |
| Goodbye | ବିଦାୟ | Bidāya |

## Asking "How Are You?"

| English | Odia | Pronunciation |
|---------|------|---------------|
| How are you? (formal) | ଆପଣ କେମିତି ଅଛନ୍ତି? | Āpaṇa kemiti achanti? |
| How are you? (informal) | ତୁମେ କେମିତି ଅଛ? | Tume kemiti acha? |
| I am fine | ମୁଁ ଭଲ ଅଛି | Muṁ bhala achi |
| I am good | ମୁଁ ଠିକ୍ ଅଛି | Muṁ ṭhik achi |

## Practice Conversation

**A**: ନମସ୍କାର! ଆପଣ କେମିତି ଅଛନ୍ତି?
*(Namaskāra! Āpaṇa kemiti achanti?)*
Hello! How are you?

**B**: ନମସ୍କାର! ମୁଁ ଭଲ ଅଛି। ଆପଣ?
*(Namaskāra! Muṁ bhala achi. Āpaṇa?)*
Hello! I am fine. And you?

**A**: ମୁଁ ମଧ୍ୟ ଭଲ ଅଛି। ଧନ୍ୟବାଦ।
*(Muṁ madhya bhala achi. Dhanyabāda.)*
I am also fine. Thank you.

| Please | ଦୟାକରି | Dayākari |
| Sorry | କ୍ଷମା କରନ୍ତୁ | Kṣamā karantu |
| Excuse me | ମାଫ କରନ୍ତୁ | Māpha karantu |
| You're welcome | ସ୍ୱାଗତ | Swāgata |

## Formal vs Informal

In Odia, there's a big difference between formal and informal speech:

| Concept | Formal (ଆପଣ) | Informal (ତୁମେ/ତୁ) |
|---------|--------------|-------------------|
| "You" | ଆପଣ (āpaṇa) | ତୁମେ (tume) / ତୁ (tu) |
| Use with | Elders, strangers, respect | Friends, younger people |

> **Tip:** When in doubt, use the formal form. It's always polite!

## Meeting Someone New

| English | Odia | Pronunciation |
|---------|------|---------------|
| What is your name? | ଆପଣଙ୍କ ନାମ କ'ଣ? | Āpaṇaṅka nāma kaṇa? |
| My name is... | ମୋ ନାମ... | Mo nāma... |
| Nice to meet you | ଆପଣଙ୍କୁ ଭେଟି ଖୁସି ଲାଗିଲା | Āpaṇaṅku bheṭi khusi lāgilā |

## Exercise: Role Play

Practice this dialogue with a friend (or yourself!):

1. Greet them: ନମସ୍କାର!
2. Ask how they are: ଆପଣ କେମିତି ଅଛନ୍ତି?
3. Introduce yourself: ମୋ ନାମ [your name]
4. Say thank you: ଧନ୍ୟବାଦ!
5. Say goodbye: ବିଦାୟ!
