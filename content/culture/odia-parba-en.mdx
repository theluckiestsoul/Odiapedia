Organized by **Odia Samaj** (and styled **Odisha Parba** in the organiser's 2026 announcement), this three-day cultural extravaganza brings the essence of Odisha to New Delhi.

## What is Odia Parba?

**Odia Parba** is not just a festival; it is an emotion for every Odia living away from home. Started in **2017** in Delhi, it has grown into a large cultural congregation, with editions in Delhi, in Surat (2018) and online (2021 and 2022). The festival aims to showcase the rich heritage, timeless traditions, handloom, handicrafts, and culinary excellence of Odisha to a global audience.

The organiser promoted the 2026 edition with the line "Come Fall in Love with Odisha".

## Key Highlights of 2026 Edition

### 1. The Grand Entrance & Decor
![Odia Parba Grand Entrance](/images/odia-parba-entrance.png)

Every year, the entrance gate is a visual spectacle, often replicating famous monuments like the **Sun Temple of Konark**, **Dola Bimana**, or the **Rath of Lord Jagannath**. Inside, exquisite 3D projection mapping and art installations narrate stories of Odisha's glorious past, including the **Paika Bidroha** of 1817, which many in Odisha describe as an early war of independence.

### 2. Cultural Extravaganza
![Odissi Dance Performance](/images/odissi-dance-stage.png)

The main stage will come alive with mesmerizing performances:
- **Classical Dance:** Graceful **Odissi** performances by renowned exponents.
- **Folk Rhythms:** High-energy **Sambalpuri**, **Paika Akhada** (martial art dance), **Chhau**, and **Ghoda Nacha**.
- **Contemporary Music:** Fusion bands and Bollywood singers from Odisha will perform popular Odia hits.
- **Drama & Theatre:** Plays depicting Odia legends and mythology.

### 3. Food Court (Aha! Odisha)
For foodies, the **Food Court** is the ultimate destination. The organiser announced more than 40 food vendors serving Odia cuisine:
- **Street Food:** Dahi Bara Aloo Dum (Cuttack style), Gupchup, and Chaat.
- **Traditional Thali:** Pakhala (fermented rice), Dalma, and Besara.
- **Sweets:** The GI-tagged **Rasagola**, **Chhenapoda** (Nayagarh style), **Chhena Jhili**, and Arisa Pitha.

### 4. Handloom & Handicrafts Hata
Shop for the finest crafts directly from Odia artisans:
- **Handlooms:** Sambalpuri Ikat, Bomkai, Maniabandha, and Kotpad sarees.
- **Handicrafts:** Pattachitra paintings, Silver Filigree (Tarakasi) from Cuttack, Stone carvings, and Applique work from Pipili.
