# Rath Yatra — The Grand Chariot Festival

**Rath Yatra** (ରଥଯାତ୍ରା) is one of the oldest and largest religious festivals in the world. Held annually in **Puri, Odisha**, it attracts millions of devotees who come to witness Lord Jagannath's journey through the streets on a massive wooden chariot.

## The Three Chariots

Three enormous chariots carry the deities from Jagannath Temple to Gundicha Temple:

| Chariot | Deity | Height (approx.) | Wheels | Color |
|---------|-------|--------|--------|-------|
| **Nandighosa** | Lord Jagannath | about 44–45 feet | 16 | Red & Yellow |
| **Taladhwaja** | Lord Balabhadra | about 43–45 feet | 14 | Red & Bluish-green |
| **Darpadalana** | Goddess Subhadra | about 42–44 feet | 12 | Red & Black |

Published heights differ slightly between sources; all agree that Nandighosa is the tallest and Darpadalana the shortest.

> "The word 'Juggernaut' in English comes from 'Jagannath' — showing the global impact of this festival."

## The Journey

The Rath Yatra covers a distance of about **3 kilometers** from the main temple to Gundicha Temple, where the deities stay for seven days before returning.

### Key Events:
1. **Snana Yatra** — Bathing ceremony on Jyeshtha Purnima, a little over two weeks before
2. **Anasara** — Deities fall "ill" and are hidden
3. **Nabajauban Darshan** — Deities appear in new form
4. **Rath Yatra** — Main chariot procession
5. **Bahuda Yatra** — Return journey on Ashadha Shukla Dashami, after the seven-day stay at Gundicha

## The Chariots

Each chariot is **newly built every year** from scratch using:
- Wood from specified tree species such as phassi and dhausa
- Traditional measurements and methods handed down among hereditary craftsmen
- Covered with colorful cloth canopies

Construction traditionally begins on **Akshaya Tritiya**, about **2 months** before the festival, and involves hundreds of hereditary carpenters and other craftsmen.

## Significance

### Religious
- According to tradition, Lord Jagannath visits his aunt's home (Gundicha Temple)
- Many devotees believe that pulling the chariot ropes brings spiritual merit and liberation (moksha)
- Everyone, regardless of caste, can participate

### Cultural
- Demonstrates Odishan craftsmanship
- Unites millions in shared devotion
- Inspiration for Rath Yatras worldwide (including ISKCON)

## When Is It Celebrated?

Rath Yatra falls on the **2nd day of Shukla Paksha** (bright fortnight) in the month of **Ashadha** (June-July) according to the Hindu calendar.

## Famous Traditions

- **Chhera Pahanra** — The Gajapati Maharaja of Puri sweeps the chariot platforms with a golden broom
- **Pahandi** — Rocking procession of deities to chariots
- **Adhara Pana** — Offering of a sweet drink to the deities on their chariots after the return journey

## Rath Yatra Around the World

ISKCON (International Society for Krishna Consciousness) organizes Rath Yatra festivals in:
- New York
- London
- San Francisco
- And many other cities worldwide
