# Jagannath Panjika — The Temple Calendar

**Jagannath Panjika** (ଜଗନ୍ନାଥ ପଞ୍ଜିକା) is the official almanac published by the **Shree Jagannath Temple Administration, Puri**. It is the most authoritative Hindu calendar followed across coastal Odisha and for all temple rituals.

## Quick Facts

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Publisher** | Shree Jagannath Temple Administration |
| **Location** | Puri, Odisha |
| **Language** | Odia |
| **Used For** | Temple rituals, festival dates, muhurta |
| **Follows** | Suryasiddhanta calculations |

## What It Contains

### Daily Panchanga

The Jagannath Panjika provides daily:

- **ତିଥି (Tithi)** — Lunar day
- **ନକ୍ଷତ୍ର (Nakshatra)** — Lunar constellation
- **ଯୋଗ (Yoga)** — Auspicious/inauspicious period
- **କରଣ (Karana)** — Half of a tithi
- **ରାହୁକାଳ (Rahu Kala)** — Inauspicious time
- **ସୂର୍ଯ୍ୟୋଦୟ/ଅସ୍ତ** — Sunrise/Sunset times

### Temple-Specific Information

- **ରଥଯାତ୍ରା** dates and schedules
- **ଏକାଦଶୀ** fasting days
- **ସଙ୍କ୍ରାନ୍ତି** dates
- Temple seva timings
- Mahaprasad availability

### Festival Calendar

Complete dates for:
- Snana Yatra
- Rath Yatra
- Bahuda Yatra
- Suna Besha
- Chandan Yatra
- And all major Hindu festivals

## Why Jagannath Panjika?

### Temple Authority

The Jagannath Temple has maintained its own astronomical calculations for centuries. The **gajapati** (temple priests) use this panjika exclusively for:

- Determining ritual timings
- Setting festival dates
- Calculating auspicious moments

### Regional Importance

Jagannath Panjika is preferred in:
- Puri district
- Coastal Odisha (Bhubaneswar, Cuttack, Balasore)
- Odia communities worldwide

> "When in doubt about any religious date in coastal Odisha, Jagannath Panjika is the final word."

## Differences from Other Panjikas

| Aspect | Jagannath Panjika | Other Panjikas |
|--------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Authority** | Jagannath Temple | Various |
| **Calculations** | Suryasiddhanta | May vary |
| **Festival dates** | Temple-specific | Regional |
| **Rath Yatra timing** | Definitive | Reference |

## How to Use

1. **Daily Reference** — Check tithi, nakshatra for the day
2. **Festival Planning** — Get accurate dates for festivals
3. **Muhurta** — Find auspicious times for weddings, griha pravesh
4. **Ekadashi** — Know fasting dates

## Significance

The Jagannath Panjika represents:

- **Continuity** — Centuries of astronomical tradition
- **Authority** — Official temple sanction
- **Accuracy** — Based on ancient Suryasiddhanta
- **Cultural identity** — Symbol of Odia heritage
