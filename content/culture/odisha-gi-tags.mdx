**Geographical Indication tags of Odisha** (ଓଡ଼ିଶାର ଜିଆଇ ଟ୍ୟାଗ) are legal registrations under India's Geographical Indications of Goods Act, 1999. A GI tag means a product's name can only be used for goods made in a defined area of Odisha. As of the GI Registry's state-wise list dated **31 December 2025**, Odisha has **26 registered entries**. They cover handloom textiles, handicrafts, farm produce and foods. A further GI, Araku Valley Arabica Coffee, is registered jointly with Andhra Pradesh.

## What is a GI tag?

A Geographical Indication identifies goods whose quality, reputation or character comes from where they are made. The **Geographical Indications Registry in Chennai** examines applications and enters approved names in the register. The applicant is usually a producers' body, cooperative or government agency. Producers in the area can then apply to become authorised users. Only goods from the defined area that follow the registered method may lawfully carry the name.

For a buyer, a GI name is a guarantee of origin, not of quality. It means, for example, that a sari sold as Bomkai should come from the registered Bomkai weaving area.

## How many GI tags does Odisha have?

The answer depends on how entries are counted, which is why published totals vary.

- The official list shows **26 entries** for Odisha as of 31 December 2025.
- One of these, the **Orissa Pattachitra logo**, is a separate registration of the logo for an existing GI. Counting distinct products only gives **25**.
- Some reports also add the jointly registered **Araku Valley Arabica Coffee**.

News reports at the time match the official list. PTI reported in January 2024 that seven new registrations took Odisha's total to 25. Cuttack Rupa Tarakasi in March 2024 was reported as the 26th.

## Handloom textiles

Nine of Odisha's GIs are handwoven textiles. The state handloom cooperative Boyanika says it trades in all nine. They include the tie-dyed **Orissa Ikat** and **Sambalpuri Bandha**, the temple-linked [Khandua silk](/culture/khandua-silk) of Nuapatna, the extra-weft [Bomkai saree](/culture/bomkai-saree), and [Kotpad handloom](/culture/kotpad-handloom), Odisha's first GI. For background on the wider Sambalpuri tradition, see the [Sambalpuri saree](/culture/sambalpuri-saree).

## Handicrafts

Handicraft GIs include [Pattachitra](/culture/pattachitra) cloth painting, [Pipili appliqué](/culture/pipili-applique), Konark stone carving and [Cuttack's silver filigree](/culture/tarakasi-silver-filigree). The January 2024 registrations recognised two tribal art forms from Rayagada district. These are the **Lanjia Saura wall painting** (also called *idital*) and the embroidered **Kapadaganda shawl** of the Dongria Kondh.

## Agricultural and food products

Food GIs from Odisha include the sweets [Odisha Rasagola](/food/rasagola) and [Kendrapara Rasabali](/food/rasabali), as well as **Dhenkanal Magji**, a sweet made from buffalo-milk cheese. **Odisha Khajuri Guda** is a jaggery made from date-palm sap in Gajapati district. **Similipal Kai Chutney** from Mayurbhanj is made with red weaver ants. Agricultural GIs include **Kandhamal Haladi** (turmeric), **Koraput Kalajeera** (an aromatic rice), **Nayagarh Kanteimundi** (a thorny brinjal), and the **Ganjam Kewda** flower along with its distilled essence, *rooh*.

## Crafts that are not GI-registered

Several well-known Odisha crafts were **not** on the Registry's list of Odisha GIs as of 31 December 2025. These include [Dhokra metal casting](/culture/dhokra-craft), [sabai grass craft](/culture/sabai-grass-craft) and [palm-leaf engraving](/culture/palm-leaf-engraving) as a separate craft. Products sold under these names may still be authentic handmade work. They simply have no GI protection.

## How to check a GI claim

- Search the product on the GI Registry's public search at search.ipindia.gov.in to see its status, applicant and registered area.
- Ask the seller which authorised user or cooperative made the product.
- Buy from the state cooperatives Boyanika (handloom) and Utkalika (handicrafts), or browse Odiapedia's [curated authentic Odisha products](/shop).
