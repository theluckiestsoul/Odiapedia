# Biraja Panjika — The Jajpur Almanac

**Biraja Panjika** (ବିରଜା ପଞ୍ଜିକା) is the traditional almanac published from **Jajpur**, also known as **Biraja Kshetra**. It is particularly followed in central and western Odisha regions.

## Quick Facts

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Publisher** | Biraja Temple Trust, Jajpur |
| **Origin** | Jajpur (Biraja Kshetra) |
| **Language** | Odia |
| **Region** | Central & Western Odisha |
| **Significance** | One of the oldest panjikas in Odisha |

## Biraja Kshetra — The Sacred Land

Jajpur is called **Biraja Kshetra** because:

- Home to the **Biraja Temple** (Maa Biraja)
- Ancient **Tantric shakti peetha**
- Mentioned in Puranas as a sacred crossing
- The name "Biraja" means "free from passion" (ବି + ରଜ)

> "Biraja Kshetra is where the Goddess Biraja resides as the presiding deity of liberation."

## What It Contains

### Daily Panchanga

The Biraja Panjika provides:

- **ତିଥି (Tithi)** — Lunar day with precise timing
- **ନକ୍ଷତ୍ର (Nakshatra)** — Constellation with degrees
- **ଲଗ୍ନ (Lagna)** — Ascendant calculations
- **ରାଶି (Rashi)** — Moon sign
- **ଯୋଗ (Yoga)** — Daily yoga
- **କରଣ (Karana)** — Half-tithi

### Regional Festivals

Biraja Panjika is particularly detailed on:

- **ନୁଆଖାଇ** (Nuakhai) — Western Odisha's main festival
- **ପୁଷ ପୁଣି** celebrations
- **ଶକ୍ତି ପୂଜା** — Goddess worship dates
- Regional melas and fairs

### Agricultural Calendar

Important for farmers:

- Planting seasons
- Harvest timings
- Rain predictions (traditional)
- Nakshatra-based farming

## Regional Following

Biraja Panjika is preferred in:

| Region | Districts |
|--------|-----------|
| **Central Odisha** | Jajpur, Dhenkanal, Angul |
| **Western Odisha** | Sambalpur, Bargarh, Bolangir |
| **Northern Odisha** | Keonjhar, Mayurbhanj |

## Biraja vs Jagannath Panjika

| Aspect | Biraja Panjika | Jagannath Panjika |
|--------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Origin** | Jajpur | Puri |
| **Focus** | General/Agricultural | Temple rituals |
| **Nuakhai** | Very detailed | Basic |
| **Rath Yatra** | Reference | Definitive |
| **Region** | Central/West | Coastal |

## Historical Significance

The Biraja Panjika:

- Dates back several centuries
- Maintained by traditional astronomers (ଜ୍ୟୋତିଷ)
- Preserves local Tantric traditions
- Contains unique regional festivals

## Importance in Odisha

Both Biraja and Jagannath Panjikas are respected, and together they serve:

- **Complete coverage** of Odisha's diverse regions
- **Rich tradition** of indigenous astronomy
- **Cultural preservation** of local festivals
- **Agricultural guidance** for rural communities

## The Biraja Temple

The panjika gets its name from **Maa Biraja Temple**:

- One of the ancient Shakti Peethas
- Worshipped as "Biraja" form of Durga
- Temple rebuilt in medieval period
- Annual **Chadak** and **Jhamu Yatra** festivals
