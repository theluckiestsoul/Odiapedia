Mistakes happen. When they do, we want to fix them quickly and transparently.

## How to report an error

Every article has a **Report an error** button near the bottom of the page. It opens an email with the page address already filled in. Please tell us:

- what is wrong or missing,
- what you believe is correct, and
- a source we can check — a document, book, official web page or other reliable reference.

You can also write to contact@odiapedia.com.

## What happens next

1. We acknowledge reports when we can and review them against reliable sources.
2. If the article is wrong, we correct it and update its "Last reviewed" date.
3. Significant corrections — a changed date, figure, attribution or conclusion — are noted at the end of the article.
4. If sources genuinely disagree, we may present both positions rather than choose one.

## What we do not change

We do not remove accurate, well-sourced information because it is unwelcome, and corrections cannot be bought. Requests from sponsors or partners are treated exactly like any other reader's report.
