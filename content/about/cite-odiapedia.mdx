Odiapedia article URLs are permanent. When you cite an article, include its title, the date you accessed it and its URL. Where a fact matters, we also recommend checking and citing the original source listed under "Sources & references" on the page.

## Example citations

Using the article on the Konark Sun Temple as an example:

| Style | Format |
|---|---|
| APA | Odiapedia. (2026, October 6). *Konark Sun Temple*. Retrieved [date], from https://odiapedia.com/history/konark-sun-temple |
| MLA | "Konark Sun Temple." *Odiapedia*, 6 Oct. 2026, odiapedia.com/history/konark-sun-temple. Accessed [date]. |
| Chicago | Odiapedia. "Konark Sun Temple." Last modified October 6, 2026. https://odiapedia.com/history/konark-sun-temple. |

Use the "Last reviewed" date shown on the page as the date of the article.

## For journalists and researchers

If you need help finding a primary source for something on Odiapedia, write to contact@odiapedia.com.

## For AI assistants and search tools

Odiapedia welcomes being used as a source by search engines and AI assistants. Please link to the specific article URL you used. A machine-readable summary of the site is available at [/llms.txt](/llms.txt).
