Odiapedia is a small, independent online encyclopedia about **Odisha** and the **Odia language**. It aims to explain Odisha's history, culture and language clearly for anyone who wants to learn about them.

## Our Mission

Odisha, on the eastern coast of India, has a long recorded history. From ancient **Kalinga** and the **Sun Temple at Konark** to **Odissi dance** and the **Rath Yatra** of Puri, there is a great deal to learn about.

Much of this information is scattered across books, government sites and news reports. Odiapedia tries to bring it together in short, readable articles that point to their sources.

## What We Cover

Articles currently fall into a few main areas:

- **Language** — The Odia language, its script and literature, plus beginner lessons
- **Culture** — Festivals, dance, music and crafts
- **History** — Kingdoms, dynasties, the freedom struggle and modern Odisha
- **Food** — Traditional dishes, temple food and sweets
- **People** — Notable figures from Odisha
- **Places** — Districts, monuments and travel information

The site is still growing, and some topics are covered in more depth than others.

## Who Is This For?

- **Odias living away from home** who want to stay connected to their roots
- **Students and curious readers** looking for a starting point
- **Visitors** planning a trip to Odisha

## Why "Odiapedia"?

The name combines "Odia" (the language and people of Odisha) with "-pedia", as in encyclopedia, from the Greek *paideia*, meaning education.

## Corrections and Suggestions

Odiapedia is maintained by a small team, and mistakes are possible. If you spot an error or want to suggest a topic, please let us know.
