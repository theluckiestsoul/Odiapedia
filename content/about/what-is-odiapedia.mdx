# What is Odiapedia?

Odiapedia is a comprehensive digital encyclopedia dedicated to documenting, preserving, and sharing the rich cultural heritage of **Odisha** with people around the world.

## Our Mission

Odisha, located on the eastern coast of India, has a history spanning thousands of years. From the mighty **Kalinga Empire** to the architectural marvels of the **Sun Temple at Konark**, from the classical **Odissi dance** to the sacred **Rath Yatra** of Puri — Odisha's heritage is vast and profound.

Yet, much of this knowledge remains scattered across various sources, often inaccessible to those who seek it. **Odiapedia aims to change that.**

## What We Cover

Our encyclopedia spans five major areas:

- **Language** — The classical Odia language, its script, literature, and evolution
- **Culture** — Traditions, festivals, arts, music, dance, and crafts
- **History** — Ancient kingdoms, medieval dynasties, freedom struggle, and modern Odisha
- **Food** — Traditional cuisine, temple prasad, sweets, and regional specialties
- **People** — Notable personalities who have shaped Odisha's identity

## Who Is This For?

Odiapedia is designed for:

- **Odias living away from home** who want to stay connected to their roots
- **Researchers and students** studying Odia culture, history, or language
- **Tourists** planning to visit Odisha and wanting to understand its heritage
- **Anyone curious** about one of India's most ancient and culturally rich states

## Why "Odiapedia"?

The name combines "Odia" (referring to the language and people of Odisha) with "pedia" (from the Greek *paideia*, meaning education or knowledge). 

> Odiapedia = Knowledge about everything Odia

## Join Our Journey

Odiapedia is an open project. We welcome contributions, corrections, and suggestions from anyone who shares our passion for preserving Odia heritage.
