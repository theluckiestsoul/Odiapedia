# Chilika Lake — Asia's Largest Lagoon

**Chilika Lake** (ଚିଲିକା ହ୍ରଦ) is Asia's largest brackish water lagoon and is often described as the second largest coastal lagoon in the world. Its water spread ranges from about **906 sq km in the dry season to 1,165 sq km in the monsoon**, and it straddles three districts of Odisha.

## Quick Facts

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Area** | About 1,165 sq km (monsoon) / 906 sq km (dry season) |
| **Depth** | Shallow: under 1 m in parts during the dry season to about 4 m in the monsoon |
| **Location** | Puri, Khordha, Ganjam districts |
| **Ramsar Status** | Ramsar Wetland since 1981 |
| **Islands** | Many islands, including Kalijai, Nalabana, Breakfast and Honeymoon islands |

## The Biodiversity Haven

### Migratory Birds

Chilika hosts around **a million migratory birds** in a typical winter, according to the Chilika Development Authority, from places as far away as:
- Siberia and the Lake Baikal region
- The Caspian Sea and Aral Sea regions
- Central Asia
- Ladakh and the Himalayas

Bird species include:
- **Flamingos** — Stunning pink flocks
- **White-bellied Sea Eagles**
- **Brahminy Kites**
- **Painted Storks**
- **Spoonbills**

> The best time for bird watching is **November to February**.

### Irrawaddy Dolphins

Chilika is home to one of the largest lagoon populations of **Irrawaddy dolphins**; annual counts have generally found well over a hundred animals. They are most often spotted on boat trips from:
- Satapada

### Marine Life

- Fish species — sources differ widely (the Chilika Development Authority lists 335 species; other sources give lower figures)
- Crabs and shrimp
- Prawns (major export)

## Kalijai Temple

On an island in the middle of Chilika sits the sacred **Kalijai Temple** (କାଳିଜାଇ ମନ୍ଦିର), dedicated to Goddess Kalijai.

**Legend:** According to local tradition, a young bride named Jai drowned in the lake on her way to her husband's home. She is now worshipped as Goddess Kalijai.

The temple is especially crowded during **Makar Sankranti** (January).

1. **Kalijai Island** — Sacred temple
2. **Nalabana Island** — Bird sanctuary
3. **Honeymoon Island (Barkuda)** — Scenic spot near Rambha
4. **Breakfast Island (Sankuda)** — Scenic island near Rambha

## How to Reach

### From Bhubaneswar
- **By Road:** approximately 100 km
- **Time:** 2–2.5 hours
- **Entry Points:** Satapada, Barkul, Rambha

### Best Season
- **October to March** — Pleasant weather, migratory birds
- **Avoid:** June-September (monsoon)

## Conservation Efforts

Challenges facing Chilika:
- Siltation from rivers
- Pollution
- Overfishing
- Invasive species (water hyacinth)

Chilika was placed on the Ramsar **Montreux Record** of threatened sites in 1993 and removed in 2002 after restoration work, including the opening of a new mouth to the sea in 2000.

The **Chilika Development Authority** works to:
- Monitor water quality
- Protect wildlife
- Regulate fishing
- Promote sustainable tourism

## Cultural Significance

Chilika appears in:
- Ancient texts and poetry
- Odia folklore
- Local fishing communities' way of life

The lake supports about **0.2 million fisherfolk** who depend on it for livelihood, according to the Chilika Development Authority.
