# Lingaraj Temple — The Crown of Bhubaneswar

**Lingaraj Temple** (ଲିଙ୍ଗରାଜ ମନ୍ଦିର) is the largest and most important temple in Bhubaneswar, the "Temple City of India." This magnificent 11th-century temple is dedicated to **Lord Shiva** as Tribhuvaneshwar.

## Quick Facts

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Built** | 11th century CE (present form) |
| **Dynasty** | Somavamsi Kings, with later Ganga additions |
| **Height** | 55 meters (180 feet) |
| **Deity** | Lord Shiva (Lingaraj) |
| **Style** | Kalinga Architecture |
| **Location** | Old Town, Bhubaneswar |

## Unique Deity

The Lingaraj is regarded as a **Swayambhu** (self-manifested) lingam and is worshipped as **Harihara** — a combined form of:
- **Shiva**
- **Vishnu**

This makes it sacred to both **Shaivites** and **Vaishnavites**.

## Architectural Marvel

### The Four Sections

| Section | Name | Purpose |
|---------|------|---------|
| **Vimana** | Deula | Main sanctuary (55m tall) |
| **Jagamohana** | Assembly hall | For devotees |
| **Nata Mandira** | Dance hall | Temple performances |
| **Bhoga Mandapa** | Offering hall | Food offerings |

### Kalinga Style Features

- **Curvilinear Deula** — The iconic tower
- **Intricately carved walls** — Depicting gods, goddesses, mythical creatures
- **No iron/cement** — Traditional mortar joints
- **Khondalite and laterite** — The temple is built of khondalite sandstone, with a laterite compound wall

## Temple Complex

The complex includes over a hundred smaller shrines around the main temple (sources give figures from **108 to about 150**), including:
- **Parvati (Bhagavati) Temple**
- **Gopalini Temple**
- **Bhavani Temple**

## Festivals

| Festival | Month | Celebration |
|----------|-------|-------------|
| **Shivaratri** | February/March | Biggest celebration |
| **Ashokastami** | March/April | Chariot procession |
| **Chandan Yatra** | April/May | 22-day sandalwood festival at Bindu Sagar |

### Rukuna Rath Yatra

During **Ashokastami**, the deity is taken on a chariot (rath) procession — a smaller version of Puri's Rath Yatra.

## Entry Rules

> **Note:** Only Hindus are allowed inside the temple.

Non-Hindus can view the temple from a **raised viewing platform** beside the compound wall.

### Dress Code
- Traditional attire preferred
- Remove footwear at entrance
- Photography not allowed inside

## Bindu Sarovar

Near the temple is **Bindu Sarovar** (Bindu Sagar), the sacred tank believed to contain waters from all holy rivers. Devotees take a dip before worship.

## History

- **6th–7th century (per some texts):** Some scholars and later Sanskrit texts suggest an earlier shrine stood here
- **11th century:** Current structure built; traditionally attributed to Somavamsi King Jajati Keshari, though historians debate which Somavamsi ruler began it
- **Later additions:** The Bhoga Mandapa and other halls were added in the 12th century and after

## Significance

Lingaraj Temple represents:
- The **zenith of Kalinga architecture**
- A **living temple** with continuous worship for about 1,000 years
- The **heart of Bhubaneswar's** spiritual identity
