**Odisha** (ଓଡ଼ିଶା), known until 2011 as **Orissa**, is a state on the eastern coast of India, on the Bay of Bengal. Its capital is **Bhubaneswar**, its official language is **Odia**, and it was formed as a separate province on **1 April 1936**. The 2011 Census counted **41,974,218** people in an area of **155,707 sq km**, divided today into **30 districts**.

This page collects the most-asked facts in one place. Figures are from the 2011 Census unless stated, since no later census results had been published at the time of writing.

## Odisha quick facts

|---|---|
| Name | Odisha (ଓଡ଼ିଶା); Orissa until 2011 |
| Name origin | From the ancient Odra people and region (Odra Desa, Odra Vishaya) |
| Older names for the region | Kalinga, Utkala, Odra |
| Capital | Bhubaneswar |
| Former capital | Cuttack (capital of the province from 1936) |
| Formed | 1 April 1936, as the province of Orissa |
| Official language | Odia (English is also used officially) |
| High Court | Orissa High Court, Cuttack |
| Legislature | Unicameral; 147-member Legislative Assembly |
| Seats in Parliament | 21 Lok Sabha, 10 Rajya Sabha |
| Chief Minister | Mohan Charan Majhi (since 12 June 2024) |
| State song | Bande Utkala Janani, by Laxmikanta Mohapatra |
| State day | Utkal Divas (Odisha Day), 1 April |

### Major rivers of Odisha

| River | Notes |
|---|---|
| Mahanadi | Odisha's largest river, about 900 km long; rises in Chhattisgarh, is dammed at Hirakud near Sambalpur, flows past Cuttack and forms a large delta on the coast |
| Brahmani | Flows through north-central Odisha to the coast; its delta adjoins those of the Mahanadi and Baitarani |
| Baitarani | Northern Odisha; flows past Jajpur to the coast |
| Subarnarekha | Enters Odisha from Jharkhand in the far north and reaches the sea in Balasore district |
| Budhabalanga | Flows through Mayurbhanj and Balasore districts to the Bay of Bengal |
| Rushikulya | Southern Odisha; reaches the sea in Ganjam district |

## Administration: 30 districts

Odisha has **30 districts**, grouped into three revenue divisions of ten districts each.

| Revenue division | Headquarters | Districts |
|---|---|---|
| Central | Cuttack | Balasore, Bhadrak, Cuttack, Jagatsinghpur, Jajpur, Kendrapara, Khordha, Mayurbhanj, Nayagarh, Puri |
| Northern | Sambalpur | Angul, Bargarh, Balangir, Deogarh, Dhenkanal, Jharsuguda, Kendujhar, Sambalpur, Subarnapur, Sundargarh |
| Southern | Berhampur | Boudh, Gajapati, Ganjam, Kalahandi, Kandhamal, Koraput, Malkangiri, Nabarangpur, Nuapada, Rayagada |

When the province was formed in 1936 it had six districts: Cuttack, Puri, Balasore, Sambalpur, Koraput and Ganjam. Princely states were merged after independence, and a reorganisation between October 1992 and January 1994 split larger districts to create the present 30. Browse all of them on the [districts of Odisha](/districts) page.

*Bande Utkala Janani* was written by Laxmikanta Mohapatra in 1912 and was formally given the status of state anthem by the Odisha Cabinet on 7 June 2020.
