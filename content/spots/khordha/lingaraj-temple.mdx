# Lingaraj Temple: The Pinnacle of Kalinga Architecture

The **Lingaraj Temple** is an ancient temple dedicated to **Lord Shiva** and is the largest temple in Bhubaneswar, the capital city of Odisha. It represents the quintessence of Kalinga architecture and culminating the medieval stages of the architectural tradition at Bhubaneswar.

## Significance
The temple is believed to have been built by the kings from the **Somavamsi dynasty**, with later additions from the Ganga rulers. The temple is built in the **Deula** style that has four components:
1.  **Vimana** (The structure containing the sanctum)
2.  **Jagamohana** (Assembly hall)
3.  **Natamandira** (Festival hall)
4.  **Bhoga-mandapa** (Hall of offerings)

The presiding deity is worshipped as **Harihara** (a combined form of Vishnu and Shiva), highlighting the cultural synthesis of Shaivism and Vaishnavism in Odisha.
