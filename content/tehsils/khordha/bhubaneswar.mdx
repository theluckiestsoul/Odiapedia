## At a Glance
Bhubaneswar is the administrative capital and largest city of Odisha. It constitutes a key tehsil within the Khordha district.

## The Heritage Diary
Known as the "Temple City," Bhubaneswar was once the capital of ancient Kalinga. It is home to over 500 temples, ranging from the 6th to the 13th century CE.

## Local Landmarks
- **Lingaraj Temple**: The largest temple in the city.
- **Dhauli Giri**: Site of the Kalinga War and peace pagoda.
- **Khandagiri & Udayagiri Caves**: Ancient Jain rock-cut shelters.

## Taste of the Land
Famous for its street food, including *Dahibara Aloodum* and world-class *Rasagola* from nearby Pahala.

## Local Heroes
Bhubaneswar has been the home of many poets, politicians, and freedom fighters who shaped modern Odisha.
