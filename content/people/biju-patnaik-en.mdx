# Biju Patnaik: The Eagle of Odisha

**Bijayananda Patnaik**, affectionately known as **Biju Babu**, was not just a politician but a phenomenon. A daredevil pilot, a freedom fighter, and the architect of modern Odisha, his life story reads like an adventure novel.

## The Daredevil Pilot

Before entering politics, Biju Patnaik was an ace pilot. 

*   **Saving Indonesian Leaders**: In 1947, on Prime Minister Nehru's request, he flew a Dakota aircraft into Dutch-besieged Indonesia to fly out Indonesian leaders, including Premier **Sutan Sjahrir** (accounts also name Vice President **Mohammad Hatta**). For this act, he was given honorary citizenship of Indonesia and the **"Bhumiputra"** (Son of the Soil) honour, and in 1995 the **Bintang Jasa Utama**.
*   **Freedom Struggle**: During the Quit India Movement he helped underground leaders such as Ram Manohar Lohia and Jayaprakash Narayan, distributed political leaflets, and was imprisoned by the British.

## Architect of Modern Odisha

As the Chief Minister of Odisha (1961-1963 and 1990-1995), he laid the foundation for the state's industrial growth. He is credited with the establishment of:

*   **Paradip Port**: Odisha's gateway to global trade.
*   **HAL Sunabeda**: Bringing aerospace industry to Koraput.
*   **Regional Engineering College, Rourkela** (now NIT Rourkela): Technical education for the state.
*   **Kalinga Prize**: An international award for the popularization of science, which he instituted in 1951 and entrusted to UNESCO.

## Legacy

Biju Babu was known for his fearlessness and deep love for Odisha. His legacy continues to inspire generations to dream big and act fearlessly.
