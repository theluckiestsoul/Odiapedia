# Biju Patnaik: The Eagle of Odisha

**Bijayananda Patnaik**, affectionately known as **Biju Babu**, was not just a politician but a phenomenon. A daredevil pilot, a freedom fighter, and the architect of modern Odisha, his life story reads like an adventure novel.

## The Daredevil Pilot

Before entering politics, Biju Patnaik was an ace pilot. 

*   **Saving Indonesian Leaders**: In 1947, on Prime Minister Nehru's request, he flew a Dakota aircraft into Dutch-besieged Indonesia to rescue Vice President **Mohammad Hatta** and Premier **Sutan Sjahrir**. For this heroic act, he was given honorary citizenship of Indonesia and the title **"Bhumiputra"** (Son of the Soil).
*   **Freedom Struggle**: He dropped political leaflets for freedom fighters and was imprisoned by the British during the Quit India Movement.

## Architect of Modern Odisha

As the Chief Minister of Odisha (1961-1963 and 1990-1995), he laid the foundation for the state's industrial growth. His vision led to the establishment of:

*   **Paradip Port**: Odisha's gateway to global trade.
*   **HAL Sunabeda**: Bringing aerospace industry to Koraput.
*   **Rourkela Steel Plant**: Industrialization of the tribal belt.
*   **Kalinga Prize**: An international award for the popularization of science, entrusted to UNESCO.

## Legacy

Biju Babu was known for his fearlessness and deep love for Odisha. He famously said, *"To be born poor is not a crime, but to remain so is."* His legacy continues to inspire generations to dream big and act fearlessly.
