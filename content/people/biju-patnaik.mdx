# Biju Patnaik — The Legendary Leader

**Biju Patnaik** (1916-1997) was a larger-than-life figure — a daredevil pilot, freedom fighter, industrialist, and visionary politician. He served as **Chief Minister of Odisha** twice and is credited with modernizing the state.

## Quick Facts

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Full Name** | Bijayananda Patnaik |
| **Birth** | March 5, 1916, Cuttack |
| **Death** | April 17, 1997, Delhi |
| **Wife** | Gyan Patnaik |
| **Children** | Naveen Patnaik (current CM), Prem, Gita |

## The Fearless Pilot

Before politics, Biju Patnaik was a legendary aviator:

### World War II
- Flew for the Royal Air Force
- Transported troops and supplies
- Known for impossible missions

### Indonesian Independence
In 1947, he secretly flew **Indonesia's first Prime Minister Sutan Sjahrir** and other leaders out of Dutch-occupied territory:

> "He risked his life flying through enemy fire to help Indonesia gain freedom."

For this, Indonesia awarded him the **Bintang Jasa Utama** (highest civilian honor for a foreigner).

### Kashmir Operations
During the 1947 Kashmir conflict, he airlifted Indian troops in civilian aircraft to defend Srinagar.

## Political Career

### First Term as Chief Minister (1961-1963)

Key achievements:
- Established **Paradeep Port** — Odisha's gateway to the world
- Founded **Regional Engineering College** (now NIT Rourkela)
- Built **Express Highways**
- Started **Kalinga Airlines**
- Established **Kalinga Tubes** (industrialization)

### Second Term (1990-1995)

At age 74, returned to transform Odisha:
- **Panchayati Raj reforms** — Power to villages
- **33% reservation for women** in local bodies
- Educational expansion
- Industrial development

## The Industrialist

Biju Patnaik founded:
- **Kalinga Airlines** — Odisha's first airline
- **Kalinga Tubes**
- **Kalinga Iron Works**
- **Kalinga Refractories**

He believed in **self-reliance** and wanted Odisha to industrialize without depending on the center.

## Philosophy

His famous beliefs:

> "If you don't take risks, you won't achieve anything."

> "Odisha has everything — minerals, coast, fertile land. What it lacks is willpower."

> "Women's empowerment is not charity, it's necessity."

## Legacy

### Named After Him
- **Biju Patnaik International Airport**, Bhubaneswar
- **Biju Expressway**
- **Biju Patnaik University of Technology**
- Countless schools, stadiums, and roads

### Political Legacy
His son **Naveen Patnaik** has been Chief Minister since 2000, making the Patnaik family the most influential in Odishan politics.

### International Recognition
- **Indonesia** — National Hero status
- **India** — Padma Bhushan (declined)
- **USSR** — Order of Lenin (for flying missions)

## Famous Story

When asked why he flew dangerous missions, he allegedly said:

> "I fly because the ground is boring."

## Remembering Biju Babu

Every year on **March 5**, Odisha celebrates **Biju Jayanti**:
- Schools and offices commemorate him
- Political leaders pay tribute
- Stories of his courage are retold
