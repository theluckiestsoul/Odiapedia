# Biju Patnaik — The Legendary Leader

**Biju Patnaik** (1916-1997) was a larger-than-life figure — a daredevil pilot, freedom fighter, industrialist, and visionary politician. He served as **Chief Minister of Odisha** twice and is credited with modernizing the state.

## The Fearless Pilot

Before politics, Biju Patnaik was a legendary aviator:

### World War II
- Flew transport aircraft with the Royal Indian Air Force
- Transported troops and supplies, including evacuation flights from Rangoon and supply missions to Soviet forces
- Known for daring missions

### Indonesian Independence
In July 1947, at Jawaharlal Nehru's request, he flew into Dutch-blockaded Java and flew **Indonesian Prime Minister Sutan Sjahrir** and other leaders out to India.

For this, Indonesia granted him honorary citizenship and the **Bhumi Putra** honour, and in 1995 awarded him the **Bintang Jasa Utama**.

### Kashmir Operations
On 27 October 1947, he flew the first airlift sortie from Delhi to Srinagar, carrying soldiers of the Sikh Regiment to defend the city.

## Political Career

### First Term as Chief Minister (1961-1963)

Key achievements credited to him:
- Established **Paradeep Port** — Odisha's gateway to the world
- Founded **Regional Engineering College** (now NIT Rourkela)
- Built the **Express Highway** to Paradip
- Brought **HAL's Sunabeda** aero-engine factory to Koraput

### Second Term (1990-1995)

At age 74, returned to transform Odisha:
- **Panchayati Raj reforms** — Power to villages
- **Reservation for women** in local bodies
- Educational expansion
- Industrial development

## The Industrialist

Biju Patnaik founded:
- **Kalinga Airlines** — Odisha's first airline
- **Kalinga Tubes**
- **Kalinga Iron Works**
- **Kalinga Refractories**

In 1951 he also funded the **Kalinga Prize**, an international award for the popularization of science administered by UNESCO.

He believed in **self-reliance** and wanted Odisha to industrialize without depending on the center.

## Philosophy

Biju Patnaik spoke often of Odisha's untapped potential and of the role of young people in the state's future. In 1992 he said that in his dream of the 21st century for the state, he would have young men and women who put the interest of the state before themselves.

## Legacy

### Named After Him
- **Biju Patnaik International Airport**, Bhubaneswar
- **Biju Expressway**
- **Biju Patnaik University of Technology**
- Countless schools, stadiums, and roads

### Political Legacy
His son **Naveen Patnaik** founded the Biju Janata Dal, named after him, and served as Chief Minister from 2000 to 2024, the longest tenure in Odisha's history.

### International Recognition
- **Indonesia** — Honorary citizenship, Bhumi Putra title, Bintang Jasa Utama (1995)

## Remembering Biju Babu

Every year on **March 5**, Odisha marks his birth anniversary, observed as **Panchayati Raj Divas**:
- Schools and offices commemorate him
- Political leaders pay tribute
- Stories of his courage are retold
