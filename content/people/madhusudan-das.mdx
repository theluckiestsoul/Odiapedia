# Madhusudan Das — The Father of Odia Nationalism

**Madhusudan Das** (ମଧୁସୂଦନ ଦାସ, 1848–1934) is the towering figure of modern Odia history. Known as **"Madhu Barrister"** (ମଧୁ ବ୍ୟାରିଷ୍ଟର), he was Odisha's **first graduate**, a pioneering lawyer and legislator — and the man who planted the seed for a **separate Odisha province**.

| **Death** | February 4, 1934, Cuttack |

| **Birthday** | 28 April, observed as **Lawyers' Day** in Odisha |

## Early Life & Education

Madhusudan Das was born **Gobinda Ballabha Choudhury** in Satyabhamapur near Cuttack:

- Converted to **Christianity** in 1866 and took the name Madhusudan Das, for which he faced a social boycott in his village
- Passed his matriculation from Cuttack in 1864
- **First Odia** to earn a BA (1870), followed by an MA (1873) from Calcutta University
- Earned a law degree (BL) from Calcutta University in 1878
- Returned to Cuttack to practice law in **1881**

## The Movement for a Separate Odisha

This was Madhusudan Das's greatest mission. In the early 1900s, **Odia-speaking areas** were divided among three British provinces:

| Province | Odia Areas Included |
|----------|-------------------|
| **Bengal** | Cuttack, Balasore, Puri |
| **Madras** | Ganjam, Koraput |
| **Central Provinces** | Sambalpur |

### The Problem
- Odia language was being **suppressed** in schools
- Bengali and Telugu were being **forced** on Odia speakers
- The Odia people had **no political voice**
- Government services used **non-Odia languages**

### His Campaign
1. **1888** — Founded the Utkal Sabha
2. **1903** — Founded the **Utkal Sammilani** to campaign for uniting all Odia-speaking areas
3. Served in legislative councils, becoming the first Odia member of both a provincial council and the central legislature
4. **1912** — Bihar and Orissa separated from Bengal as a new province (partial success)
5. **1921** — Became a minister in Bihar and Orissa under dyarchy
6. **1920s** — Continued campaigning for full separation
7. **April 1, 1936** — **Odisha became a separate province** (two years after his death)

> April 1 is celebrated as **Utkal Divas** (Odisha Day), marking the formation of the province that he had campaigned for.

## Social Reforms

Beyond politics, Madhusudan Das was a social revolutionary:

### Education
- Promoted **women's education** — revolutionary for the time
- Advocated for Odia as the **medium of instruction** in schools

### Industry
- Founded **Utkal Tannery** in Cuttack — Odisha's first modern industry
- Believed Odias must be **economically self-reliant**
- Founded **Orissa Art Wares** (1897) to promote Odisha's silver filigree craft

### Social Justice
- Fought against **caste discrimination**
- Championed the rights of **underprivileged communities**
- As a Christian, he faced prejudice but used it to challenge **social orthodoxy**

## Legacy & Honors

### Lawyers' Day (April 28)
- His birthday is observed as **Lawyers' Day** in Odisha
- On **Utkal Divas** (April 1), the anniversary of Odisha's formation, he is remembered as a leading architect of the movement

### Memorials
- **Madhusudan Das statue** — Multiple cities in Odisha
- **Madhusudan Law University**, Cuttack, named after him
- Many roads, institutions and scholarships across Odisha

### Recognition
- Considered alongside **Gopabandhu Das** and **Fakir Mohan Senapati** as the three pillars of Odia nationalism
- His legal practice set a benchmark for integrity
- Built the foundation for the **modern Odisha state**
