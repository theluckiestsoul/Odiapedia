**Gangadhar Meher** (ଗଙ୍ଗାଧର ମେହେର, 1862–1924) was an Odia poet from Barpali in western Odisha, honoured as **Swabhaba Kabi**, the poet of nature. Born into a weaver family and schooled only to the primary level, Gangadhar Meher taught himself the Sanskrit and Odia classics and wrote narrative poems, above all **Tapaswini**, that are counted among the finest works of modern Odia literature.

## Early life

According to the Odia Virtual Academy, Gangadhar Meher was born on **9 August 1862**, a Shravana Purnima day, at **Barpali** in present-day [Bargarh district](/district/bargarh). His father, **Chaitanya Meher**, was a weaver who also practised as a village vaidya and later ran a village school. The family belonged to the weaving community, and the young Gangadhar helped at the loom.

His formal education ended at the **Middle Vernacular standard**, about class five. Gangadhar Meher University notes that he was "denied the benefit of formal education" beyond this, yet through private study he acquired a deep knowledge of Sanskrit and Odia classical literature. He was married at about the age of ten, as was common at the time.

## Working life

Gangadhar Meher earned his living in modest clerical roles. The Odia Virtual Academy records that he first wrote letters and petitions for villagers, then worked as an **amin** (land measurer) under the local zamindar, Lal Nruparaj Singh, and was later promoted to **moharir** (clerk), serving at places including Sambalpur, Bijepur, Padmapur and Barpali. He wrote poetry alongside this work throughout his life.

## Poetry and themes

Gangadhar Meher wrote in the classical kavya tradition but with a fresh, clear voice. His poetry is noted for:

- **Nature:** detailed, loving descriptions of forests, rivers, seasons and village life, which earned him the title Swabhaba Kabi.
- **Moral vision:** Gangadhar Meher University describes him as a poet who "strove for truth, duty and goodness" and sought a close harmony between humanity and nature.
- **Women at the centre:** several of his major works focus on women's endurance and dignity, notably Sita in Tapaswini.
- **Synthesis:** his work bridges the older ornate style and the simpler diction of modern Odia poetry.

### Tapaswini

**Tapaswini**, his magnum opus, retells the later life of **Sita** after she is exiled by Rama, focusing on her suffering, patience and spiritual strength in Valmiki's hermitage. It is widely studied in Odisha and is regarded as one of the most important Odia kavyas of its period.

## Legacy and honours

- **Gangadhar Meher University:** the university's handbook records that Sambalpur College was founded in **1944**, renamed **Gangadhar Meher College** in **1949**, and raised to university status as **Gangadhar Meher University** on 30 May **2015**.
- **Gangadhar National Award for Poetry:** instituted by Sambalpur University in his name and given to Indian poets.
- **Western Odisha:** he is celebrated as one of the major literary figures of the Sambalpur region.
