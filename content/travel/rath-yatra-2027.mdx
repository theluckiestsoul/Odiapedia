**Rath Yatra** — the chariot festival of Lord Jagannath, Balabhadra and Subhadra in Puri — is held on the second day of the bright fortnight of Ashadha (*Ashadha Shukla Dwitiya*). In **2027 that date is Monday 5 July**, according to published calendars. Read about the festival itself in [Rath Yatra](/culture/rath-yatra).

> **Provisional dates.** The dates below are worked out from the Hindu lunar calendar. The Shree Jagannatha Temple Administration had **not** announced the official 2027 schedule when this page was last checked on 6 October 2026; we will update it when it does.

## Expected dates for 2027

| Ritual | Expected date | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Snana Purnima | about Friday 18 June 2027 | The deities are bathed in public on the Snana Bedi |
| Anasara | the following ~15 days | The deities "fall ill" and are hidden from view; painted images are worshipped instead |
| **Rath Yatra** | **Monday 5 July 2027** | The three chariots are pulled to the Gundicha Temple |
| **Bahuda Yatra** | about Tuesday 13 July 2027 | The return journey to the main temple |

Other rituals follow in order — Hera Panchami, Suna Besha, Adhara Pana and Niladri Bije — but their 2027 dates have not been published and we do not list estimates.

## The ritual sequence

1. **Snana Purnima** — the public bathing festival on Jyeshtha Purnima.
2. **Anasara** — about two weeks of seclusion; devotees worship *patta-chitra* paintings of the deities.
3. **Netrotsava / Nabajaubana darshan** — the deities reappear, renewed.
4. **Rath Yatra** — the chariots **Nandighosha** (Jagannath), **Taladhwaja** (Balabhadra) and **Darpadalana** (Subhadra) are pulled along the Bada Danda to the Gundicha Temple.
5. **Hera Panchami** — Goddess Lakshmi visits Gundicha in search of Jagannath.
6. **Bahuda Yatra** — the return journey.
7. **Suna Besha** — the deities are adorned in gold on their chariots.
8. **Adhara Pana** — a sweet drink is offered to the deities on the chariots.
9. **Niladri Bije** — Jagannath re-enters the main temple and returns to the Ratna Simhasana.

## How Rath Yatra 2026 went

In 2026 an extra (*adhika*) lunar month pushed the festival late: Snana Purnima was on 29 June, **Rath Yatra on Thursday 16 July**, Bahuda Yatra on 24 July and Niladri Bije on the night of 27–28 July.

## Tips for visitors

- Book accommodation in Puri months ahead; prices rise sharply around the festival.
- Expect huge crowds, heat and humidity, and monsoon rain. Carry water, wear light traditional clothes and keep valuables to a minimum.
- Follow police and temple directions on the Bada Danda; access is tightly controlled near the chariots.
- Temple rules on dress and phones apply during the festival too — see [Jagannath Temple timings and rules](/travel/jagannath-temple-puri-timings-rules).
