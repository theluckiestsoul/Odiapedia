The **best time to visit Odisha** (ଓଡ଼ିଶା) is from **October to March**, when the weather is cool and mostly dry across the coast, the temple towns and the hills. Odisha has a tropical monsoon climate: a hot pre-monsoon season from March to May, a wet south-west monsoon from June to September that brings most of the year's rain, and a pleasant post-monsoon and winter season. The right month also depends on what you want to see, because Odisha's biggest festivals and its wildlife seasons are spread through the year.

## Odisha's seasons at a glance

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) divides the year into winter (January and February), the pre-monsoon or hot season (March to May), the south-west monsoon (June to September) and the post-monsoon season (October to December). Odisha Tourism points out that every season has something to offer: winters bring festivals and events, while summer can suit beaches and hill stations.

### Winter (November to February): the main travel season

Days are mild and sunny and nights cool. In Bhubaneswar, IMD data show January as the coolest month, with a daily mean of about 22 C. This is the best time for sightseeing in [Bhubaneswar](/travel/bhubaneswar), [Puri](/travel/puri) and [Konark](/travel/konark), for birdwatching on [Chilika Lake](/travel/chilika-lake-travel-guide) and for the hills, where [Daringbadi](/travel/daringbadi) and [Koraput](/travel/koraput) can be cold at night. Hotels are busiest around Christmas and New Year, so book early.

### Summer (March to May): hot and humid

Temperatures rise steeply from March. May is usually the hottest month on the plains; Bhubaneswar's IMD-recorded maximum is 46.7 C (5 June 2012). Heatwaves are common in western and interior districts. If you travel now, plan sightseeing for early morning and evening, carry water, and consider the hills. Odisha Tourism suggests beaches and hill stations for summer breaks.

### Monsoon (June to September): green and wet

The south-west monsoon usually reaches Odisha in June. According to IMD normals, June to September brings about 1,150 mm of rain, roughly four-fifths of the annual total. Landscapes turn green and waterfalls are at their fullest, but roads can flood, seas are rough and some forest areas close or restrict entry. This is also the season of the [Rath Yatra](/culture/rath-yatra) in Puri.

### Post-monsoon (October): a good shoulder month

Rain tapers off in October and the countryside is still green, making it a good month for waterfalls in the hills. October is also part of the cyclone season on the coast, so watch IMD forecasts.

## Coast, hills or wildlife: when to go where

### The coast (Puri, Konark, Gopalpur, Chandipur)

**October to March** is best: comfortable temperatures and calmer seas. Swimming is risky at many Odisha beaches all year, and especially in the monsoon. Cyclones are most likely in April and May and from October to December. See our guides to [Gopalpur-on-Sea](/travel/gopalpur-on-sea) and [Chandipur](/travel/chandipur).

### The hills (Koraput, Daringbadi, Kandhamal)

**September to February** suits the southern and central hills. Odisha Tourism recommends October to February for Daringbadi, when night temperatures can approach freezing. The monsoon is beautiful but wet, and ghat roads can be slippery.

### Wildlife parks and wetlands (Simlipal, Bhitarkanika, Satkosia, Chilika, Debrigarh)

**November to March** is usually best for wildlife viewing and migratory birds. Several protected areas close or limit entry for part of the year, for example during the monsoon or the breeding season, and the dates are set by the Odisha Forest Department each year. Check before you plan: see [Simlipal](/travel/simlipal-national-park), [Bhitarkanika](/travel/bhitarkanika-national-park) and [Satkosia](/travel/satkosia).

### Western Odisha (Sambalpur, Hirakud)

**October to February** is the comfortable window. Summers in the west are among the hottest in the state. Odisha Tourism recommends October to February for [Hirakud Dam](/travel/hirakud-dam).
