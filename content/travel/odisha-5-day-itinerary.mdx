This **Odisha 5-day itinerary** (ଓଡ଼ିଶା ପାଞ୍ଚ ଦିନର ଭ୍ରମଣ ଯୋଜନା) extends the classic Bhubaneswar, Puri and Konark circuit with a boat day on Chilika Lake and time in the craft villages of Pipili and Raghurajpur. It suits first-time visitors who want temples, crafts and some nature without long drives, and it is most comfortable from October to March; November to February is the main season for migratory birds on Chilika.

## Itinerary at a glance

| Day | Route | Highlights | Overnight |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bhubaneswar | Lingaraj, Mukteswar and Rajarani temples, Udayagiri and Khandagiri caves | Bhubaneswar |
| 2 | Bhubaneswar to Dhauli, Pipili and Puri | Ashokan edicts, appliqué workshops, Puri beach at sunset | Puri |
| 3 | Puri and Raghurajpur | Jagannath Temple, Pattachitra and palm-leaf artists | Puri |
| 4 | Puri to Satapada (Chilika) and back | Boat trip, Irrawaddy dolphins, lagoon islands | Puri |
| 5 | Puri to Konark and Bhubaneswar | Konark Sun Temple, Chandrabhaga beach | Bhubaneswar |

## Day 1: Bhubaneswar, the temple city

Begin at the [Lingaraj Temple](/history/lingaraj-temple), then visit the Mukteswar and Rajarani temples nearby, both noted for their carving. After lunch, climb the Udayagiri and Khandagiri caves, Jain rock-cut cells on two low hills on the city's western side.

**Food to try:** a full Odia meal with dalma and seasonal fish curry.

**Practical tips:**
- Non-Hindus are not permitted inside the Lingaraj Temple's sanctum; a designated viewing platform outside is available to them.
- If you add Nandankanan Zoological Park, avoid Mondays: the zoo and its Botanical Garden close every Monday, according to the official website.

## Day 2: Dhauli and Pipili on the way to Puri

Stop first at **Dhauli**, approximately 8 km south of Bhubaneswar, to see Emperor Ashoka's rock edicts and the rock-cut elephant, then the Peace Pagoda on the hilltop. Continue to **Pipili**, approximately 18 km from Bhubaneswar, where workshops make brightly coloured [Pipili appliqué](/culture/pipili-applique) for temple festivals and homes. Puri is approximately 60 to 65 km from Bhubaneswar in all. Check in and walk on the beach in the evening.

**Food to try:** fresh sea fish at the Puri seafront.

**Practical tips:**
- Buy appliqué directly from workshops if possible and ask about the materials; quality varies.
- Swim only at guarded stretches of beach.

## Day 3: Jagannath Temple and Raghurajpur

Visit the [Jagannath Temple](/history/jagannath-temple) early in the morning. In the afternoon, drive to **Raghurajpur**, approximately 12 to 14 km from Puri, a village of painters known for [Pattachitra](/culture/pattachitra) on cloth, palm-leaf etching, papier-mâché masks and the Gotipua dance tradition. Artists often show visitors how the natural colours are prepared.

**Food to try:** Mahaprasad from the Ananda Bazar inside the temple complex, for those permitted entry, and [rasagola](/food/rasagola).

**Practical tips:**
- Entry to the Jagannath Temple is restricted to Hindus.
- A dress code has applied since 1 January 2024: shorts, half pants, torn jeans, skirts and sleeveless clothes are not permitted.
- Mobile phones and cameras are prohibited inside the temple.

## Day 4: Chilika Lake from Satapada

Leave Puri early for **Satapada**, approximately 50 km away on the eastern side of [Chilika Lake](/history/chilika-lake), the large brackish-water lagoon that became India's first Ramsar wetland in 1981. Boats from Satapada head towards the lake's mouth, where Irrawaddy dolphins are often seen, and can include a stop at Rajhans island, which Odisha Tourism describes as a virgin island in the lagoon. Return to Puri in the afternoon.

**Food to try:** Chilika prawns or crab, cooked in Odia style at Satapada.

**Practical tips:**
- Boats are run by local boat associations, with fares varying by boat size and duration. Agree the route and duration before boarding.
- Mornings are calmer and better for dolphin sightings. Ask the boatman to keep a respectful distance from the dolphins and to avoid chasing them.
- Carry a hat and sunscreen; there is little shade on the water.

## Day 5: Konark and back to Bhubaneswar

Drive approximately 35 km along the coast from Puri to the [Konark Sun Temple](/history/konark-sun-temple), the 13th-century chariot-shaped temple of the Sun god and a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1984. Walk to nearby Chandrabhaga beach if time allows, then continue approximately 65 km to Bhubaneswar for your onward journey.

**Food to try:** chhena jhili from sweet stalls along the Konark to Pipili road.

**Practical tips:**
- The Sun Temple is an ASI-protected, ticketed monument. Check the ASI website for current timings and fees.
- Leave enough buffer for the drive to the airport or railway station, as traffic can slow the last stretch into Bhubaneswar.

## Customise this trip

To slow the pace, add a night at Konark or a second day in Puri. Birdwatchers visiting in winter can swap the Satapada boat trip for Mangalajodi on Chilika's northern edge.
