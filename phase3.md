# Phase 3 – Actionable Steps

**Theme:** Content depth + SEO + structure
**Goal:** Make Odiapedia useful, discoverable, and credible

---

## Phase 3 Objective (Lock This In)

By the end of Phase 3:

* Odiapedia feels *content-rich*
* Google starts indexing pages
* Users can explore naturally
* Writing & publishing feels smooth

No infra. No automation. No distractions.

---

## STEP 1: Stabilize the MDX System

### Action Items

* [ ] Finalize MDX folder structure
* [ ] Ensure all articles render correctly
* [ ] Standardize frontmatter fields:

  * title
  * description
  * category
  * slug

### Deliverable

* You trust the content system
* No surprises while publishing

---

## STEP 2: Publish Core Content (Most Important)

### Target

📌 **15–20 total articles**

### Distribution (Recommended)

* Language: 5
* Culture: 5
* History: 4
* Food: 3
* People: 2–3

### Rules

* One article = one topic
* Clear headings
* Simple explanations
* Internal links where relevant

### Deliverable

* Site no longer feels empty
* Visitors spend time reading

---

## STEP 3: Improve Category Pages

### Action Items

* [ ] Each category page should:

  * Explain what the category is about
  * List all related articles
* [ ] Add short intro text for SEO

Example:

> “This section covers major festivals, traditions, and cultural practices of Odisha.”

### Deliverable

* Better navigation
* Better SEO signals

---

## STEP 4: Internal Linking Strategy

### Action Items

* [ ] Link related articles inside content
* [ ] Add “Related articles” section at bottom
* [ ] Avoid orphan pages (no page without links)

### Deliverable

* Strong site structure
* Google understands topic clusters

---

## STEP 5: SEO Foundations (No Overkill)

### Action Items

* [ ] Page titles from frontmatter
* [ ] Meta descriptions from frontmatter
* [ ] One `<h1>` per article
* [ ] Clean URLs
* [ ] Alt text for images

### Deliverable

* Pages are search-ready
* Social previews look correct

---

## STEP 6: Sitemap & Indexing

### Action Items

* [ ] Generate `sitemap.xml`
* [ ] Add `robots.txt`
* [ ] Submit site to Google Search Console
* [ ] Submit sitemap manually

### Deliverable

* Google starts crawling
* You see impressions (even if clicks are low)

---

## STEP 7: Light Analytics (Optional but Useful)

### Action Items

Choose **one**:

* Google Analytics
* Plausible / Umami (privacy-friendly)

Track only:

* Page views
* Top pages
* Traffic sources

### Deliverable

* You know what content works
* No obsession with metrics

---

## STEP 8: Content Publishing Rhythm

### Action Items

* [ ] Decide a realistic cadence:

  * 2 articles / week (ideal)
  * or 1 article / week (acceptable)
* [ ] Keep a simple content backlog

### Deliverable

* Consistency
* No burnout

---

## STEP 9: Social Sharing (Low Effort)

### Action Items

* [ ] Share each new article on:

  * Instagram
  * Twitter/X
* [ ] Mix English + Odia captions
* [ ] Link back to site

### Deliverable

* Slow but steady awareness
* Brand recall builds

---

## STEP 10: Phase 3 Review Check

Phase 3 is **complete** only if you can say:

* [ ] I can publish confidently
* [ ] The site feels rich, not empty
* [ ] Google has indexed pages
* [ ] Navigation feels natural
* [ ] I know what to write next

If yes → move on.

---

## 🚫 Explicitly NOT Allowed in Phase 3

* ❌ Terraform
* ❌ Ansible
* ❌ CMS
* ❌ Database
* ❌ Comments
* ❌ Monetization
* ❌ Over-optimizing SEO
